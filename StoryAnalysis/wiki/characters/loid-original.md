## Lore & History

Loid was present when Albrecht Entrati travelled back to the year of 1999, and was given the responsibility of breaking the device used afterwards so that he could not be followed. It is unclear whether Loid was still serving the Entrati family as himself at this time or whether the Necraloid version of himself had already been created; either way, following this event, Loid was placed into cryostasis as part of the procedure of the Kalymos Sequence. The family were left unaware of his survival, believing that he had died and been turned into a Necraloid.

### Whispers in the Walls

Due to the increased threat of The Indifference, the Necraloid awakens the Kalymos Sequence and leads the Tenno down to Albrecht's Laboratories and Sanctum Anatomica, where they battle The Murmur and awaken the Sleeper - the human, original Loid - from cryostasis, revealing that the Necraloid was created to accompany and assist his human counterpart. However, the original Loid refers to the Necraloid with disdain and opts to send him back up to the Necralisk to ensure the rest of the family remains asleep and unaware of the laboratories.

Loid reveals that the Kalymos Sequence requires the Operator to use Transference on Albrecht's Vessels. However, upon doing so they find themselves within 1999, revealing that the Indifference had successfully followed Albrecht back in-time. Meanwhile in the present, the Indifference attempts to taunt Loid.

A message from Albrecht is extracted from the Transference buffer, explaining that his efforts to time travel were done to protect Loid from the Indifference, and also leaves behind a blueprint for the Jahu Gargoyle that translates the Cavia's Voidtongue. The now-capable speaking Fibonacci reveals that Loid tossed away a message for him from Albrecht's Grimoire, unwilling to read his self-justifications. The Sanctum is then under attack by The Fragmented which is made vulnerable to the contents of the recovered page: Albrecht's love and praise for Loid's servitude. A Void-controlled Vessel attempts to attack, but is placated and pacified by a Tenno-controlled Vessel.

Loid, witnessing this act of love he had long since craved from Albrecht, is determined to see things through to the end and makes preparations to send the Tenno to 1999.

### The Lotus Eaters

With the help of the Lotus, Loid discovers a connection between the Vessels and the Protoframes and is fully prepared to send the Drifter back to 1999.

### The Old Peace

Loid assists with the working of the Dark Refractory and monitors the security systems of the labs while the Lotus and the Operator dive into the latter's lost memories of Tau. When they resurface abruptly he expresses reservations about the process, encouraging the Operator to stop digging for fear of what "polluted histories" they might be left with. However, when the Operator tells him about their friend he relents and allows them to continue diving.

As The Murmur continue to assault the labs to the point of disrupting the Jahu Gargoyle's functionality and reverting the Cavia's ability of speech, he attempts again to convince the Operator to stop, reminding them when they become distressed that the events they are undergoing are only memories and the outcome cannot be changed.

The Murmur eventually succeed in breaking the lab's defenses, although the Operator is able to repel them. Loid firmly states that this will be the final time he allows them into the Dark Refractory until they have adequately rested, as the system greatly taxes the user.

### The Devil's Triad

Loid contacts the Drifter over Kinemantik Instant Messenger informing them that three new Protoframes have suddenly appeared in Sanctum Anatomica: Lyon, Marie, and Roathe.

Roathe will eventually reveal his plan to have Loid reveal himself and everything that has been happening beneath the Necralisk to the Entrati, requesting The Drifter's assistance to do it. If the Drifter is able to enlist Marie's and Lyon's help to convince Loid along with Roathe, the latter will finally make the decision to inform the family, though he fears Gomaitru and the rest will tear his limbs in a fit of a rage upon their discovery of the things happening beneath their feet and asks The Drifter for support when the time comes.

Rank 5 - Family & KIM Spoilers ▾▾

If the player enters the Necralisk after Loid sends the above message and has reached Rank 5 - Family from Entrati, a cutscene will play showing him about to re-enter the Necralisk from the hidden entrance to the Sanctum. Though he nearly abandons the attempt, Roathe threatens him into proceeding, and Loid reunites with the family. Speaking to them afterwards will have them express delight at Loid's survival and their shared disdain at Albrecht's abandonment, while Loid himself expresses his desire to continue serving the family and reaffirms that he served them not simply because it was his duty to do so, but out of genuine love.

## Trivia

- According to the lead writer of Warframe and some Kinemantik Instant Messenger conversations, Albrecht and Loid's relationship is fully romantic.
- The Welsh Loid surname comes from the well-known Welsh personal name Lloyd. This name is originally derived from the word "llwyd," which means "grey", "grey-haired; sacred". The name may originally allude to experience and wisdom, and probably denoted a person entitled to respect.
- Though Loid is portrayed with a septum piercing in every other appearance, it is missing in his regular overworld model as well as during Whispers in the Walls cutscenes. It was originally present in said cutscenes, but was then removed in Hotfix 35.0.4 (2023-12-20), with the patch notes stating this was a "fix".
