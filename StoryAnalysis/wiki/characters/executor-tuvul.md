## Lore & History

### Zariman Ten Zero

Tuvul was the sponsor of Yonta, the Archimedean that designed the Zariman Ten-Zero.

Hidden passages and other secret areas of the Zariman Ten Zero tileset may contain an interactable computer tablet that broadcast holographic messages of Tuvul's speeches to the colonists. Initially, he supported them in their mission, but as complications grew as the ship moved closer to Saturn, Tuvul became increasingly exasperated with the Zariman colonists' fears and uncertainty. Soon, frustration turned to rage and Tuvul began punishing them in retribution for the delays, which included the restriction of food rations and eventually the mass purge of the colonists' relatives in Imperial territory. After the ship was lost to the Void, Tuvul disowned any relationship with the Zariman Project and abandoned it to its fate.

After the failure of the Zariman project, Tuvul tried to have all traces of the ship's existence destroyed, including the Tenno after they were found to be still alive. Fortunately, Margulis' efforts ensured he couldn't do such thing.

The disastrous disappearance of the Zariman, combined with his mishandling of the situation severely damaged Tuvul's reputation. Roathe believed that the reason the Orokin chose Tormis to lead the first expedition to Tau instead of Tuvul was a form of mass rebuke towards his mistakes.

### Detron Crewman Synthesis

Some time before the Zariman Ten Zero incident, Tuvul could be seen overseeing the trial of Archimedean Perintol along with other Executors, including Ballas. There, he witnessed the birth of the prototype of the Sentients at the hands of said Archimedean, designed to be sent to the Tau System. Executor Tuvul did not express full disapproval toward the project.

### Kinemantik Instant Messenger

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messenger/Roathe

From Roathe's Memory Logs it's said that Tuvul was pushed away from the expedition to the Tau System due to the incident of the Zariman Ten-Zero. Once the Solar Rail to Tau was established, Executor Tormis took charge.

### Voruna's Leverian Exhibit

Tuvul was the conductor of the Circulus, the holiest Yuvan theater on Lua, where the Seven performed their Continuity. Voruna was the warden of the Circulus, but she turned against him during the fall of the Orokin Empire. Tuvul attempted to perform Continuity on an Operator to escape his demise, using Void manifestations from a grimoire created from Albrecht Entrati's understanding of the Void to safeguard himself.

The manifestations eventually overwhelmed Voruna and succeeded in killing her wolf pack. Mourning, she hijacked Tuvul's Continuity and performed it on herself, reviving her wolves and merging her and them into a singular entity, before killing Tuvul.
