## Lore & History

### Jade Shadows: Constellations

Vena arrives from the future with her mentee, Orion, to the present Origin System to capture the Stalker, as their only option to save their doomed future. Concurrently, Ryoku and Sirius, the latter also being Stalker's son due to Eternalism, arrive as well, for the same purpose. Both sides conflict over who gets to capture Stalker and take him to the future. As part of Hunhow's plan to end the son's eternal feud, Stalker ends up incapacitating both mentors in order to lure the sons together, where Hunhow sacrifices his memories to end their feud, creating Pontis Tower as a safe haven.

Despite the peace created by Hunhow, Vena's rivalry with Ryoku has not dissipated and they remain eager to fight each other again, only this time both of them excuse it as means to entertain the now-senile Sentient, with their real motivation being their love for battle.

## Trivia

- Vena appears to be romantically obsessed with Orion, showing a possessive side and refers to herself as Stalker's future daughter-in-law, as well as the depiction of the Relationship Goals Poster.

Several of her Gemini Skin voicelines are often related to money as well as a mention to "Cephalon Parvos", suggesting she may be affiliated with the Corpus.

  - Cephalon Parvos was also alluded by Hunhow during Jade Shadows: Constellations when Stalker is infiltrating her ship - The Kuva Wytch.
- Vena is the Latin word for vein and blood vessel, with the word itself being used across several languages.
