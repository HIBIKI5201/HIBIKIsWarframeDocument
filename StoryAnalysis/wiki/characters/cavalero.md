## Lore & History

### General

As a security personnel, Cavalero was tasked with maintaining peace and order aboard the Zariman Ten Zero. Parvos Granum once tried to hire Cavalero as his enforcer with a big payout, but he turned down the offer.

### The Void-Jump Accident

Prior to the Void Jump Accident, under Quinn's orders he violently rounded up those who protested against a long unsafe void jump and executed their leaders.

Cavalero was one of the few adults on the Zariman who didn't get affected and driven insane from Void exposure before their death during the Void Jump Accident. After he'd witnessed Hombask's demise by the other adults who went insane, he blew himself and the approaching attackers up with a grenade.

### Rebirth

As the Zariman reappeared through the Void, Cavalero and four others were revived through the Void's mysterious powers. Now Conceptual Embodiments of their former selves, most of their memories have eroded away. As the player ranks up in the Holdfasts syndicate, they will slowly begin to remember who they were and the events that took place before and after the void jump.
