## Lore

### The Old Peace

Valdur worked alongside the Operator and Adis to fight off the Anarchs in The Perita Rebellion.

However, Ballas tasked Valdur to oversee the peace treaty's dissolution, including framing the Operator and Adis as the saboteurs of the World-Seeder. Valdur admitted to the Operator that he could not bring himself to carry out this order, and was apologetic for Adis' death. The Operator instructed Valdur to bury Adis' body and to not return to the Tauron Academy, despite being explicitly told to take him back.

His final fate is unknown.

### The Perita Rebellion

Like in The Old Peace quest, Valdur (along with Adis) provides commands to the Tenno as they transit through the Perita moon.

## Trivia

- Valdur's name might be based on "Baldur", a god in Germanic mythology.
- Galastra once called Valdur her "old pet", implying he may previously served under her before her defection from the Orokin.
- As a pre-Collapse Grineer, Valdur resembles to the Grineer soldiers shown in Warframe's 2019 Cinematic Trailer.
