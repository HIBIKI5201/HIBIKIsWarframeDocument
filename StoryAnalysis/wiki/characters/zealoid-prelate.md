## Trivia

Zealoid seems to be a combination of the term zealot and -oid, a suffix meaning "resembling" or "having the likeness".

  - This probably refers to the fact that Zealoid is a new kind of being with a resemblance to Zealot.
- Prior to Update 29.0 (2020-08-25) and after the end of Nightwave Series 2 players needed to craft the Orokin Derelict Emissary Assassination key to access the boss fight. The reusable blueprint for the key was purchasable from the Market for 2,500. The key requirement was removed in Hotfix 28.3.3 (2020-08-13).
- Manufacturing Requirements
- 7,500 / 10 / 4,000 / 1,000 / 100 / Time: 1 hrs
- Rush: 10
- Market Price: N/A / Blueprints Price: 2,500
- A prelate is a clergyman of high rank and authority; normally a bishop.

The Zealoid Prelate and Zealoid Bastion share the same model.

  - They also bear a striking resemblance to the Stinger enemies encountered in Dark Sector, Digital Extremes' previous successful video game.
- This was the first boss to feature a permanent health bar on the player's HUD.
- Multiple Zealot Prelates can be summoned in the Simulacrum, using the same movesets as Phase 1, and depleting all health causes it to kneel down instead of despawning. To completely despawn, the player needs to press the "Kill Enemies" button on the summon menu.
