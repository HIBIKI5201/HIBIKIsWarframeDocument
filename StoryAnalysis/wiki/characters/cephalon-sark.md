## 概要

The Index の司会。Cephalon Capture にも登場

> “"This is The Index! I'm Cephalon Sark, your unbiased host!"”
> —Cephalon Sark
> Cephalon Sark is the host of The Index, and also one of the Cephalons in Conclave Cephalon Capture mode. In the latter, Sark is characterized primarily by his referring to Tenno of the opposing team as fish and insulting them in various ways based on this credence, he occasionally speaks in third-person when stating a feeling. He appears to be very loyal to his team and will call out for them to rescue him when in enemy hands.

## Trivia

- Cephalon Sark shares a few aspects with Commander Sark from the 1982 film TRON. Beyond the fact that they share names, both are self-aware AI, and both preside over games; Commander Sark and the Game Grid and Cephalon Sark with Cephalon Capture/The Index.
- Cephalon Sark is voiced by Scott Montiel.
