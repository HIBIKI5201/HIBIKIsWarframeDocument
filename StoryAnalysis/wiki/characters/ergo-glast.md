## Lore

Ergo Glast was once a Corpus scientist who was an apprentice under Frohd Bek. Under his apprenticeship, he developed the Animo Processor, a processor capable of demonstrating artificial intelligence.

### The Glast Gambit

> “The Mycona earn their living by harvesting the biological products of the Infested. A dangerous trade, and we Perrin have always given them a fair value for their goods... but I've received disturbing news. That cleric of greed, Nef Anyo, has raided their colony, making off with more than just the resources they depend on.”

> —Ergo Glast

A trading partner of The Perrin Sequence, the Mycona Colony, was raided by Nef Anyo and had their children kidnapped for trafficking. Among the kidnapped was Neewa, a child that is essential for the colony's survival because of her immunity to the Infestation. This prompted Glast to ask the Tenno for their aid in saving them.

Through a series of Index matches and wagers, the Tenno eventually won the children and Neewa back from Nef Anyo's hands.

### Operation: Ambulas Reborn

> “Fellow profiteers, this is a once-in-a-lifetime strategic investment opportunity. A game-changer in robotic combat solutions. We're taking big data from our Animo Project and doing a full-stack integration into our most lethal combat proxies. This is: AMBULAS, REBORN!”

> —Frohd Bek

At some point after Glast left the Corpus, Frohd Bek used Glast's Animo technology to enhance his Ambulas proxies. To raise funds for mass production of these new Ambulas units, Bek made an announcement throughout the Corpus faction for an investment opportunity to his Animo Project. Glast picked up on this and decided to back the project anonymously in order to gain insider info on Ambulas production. With the help of the Tenno, Glast found out the location of Bek's capital ship that hosted the Animo Processor and destroyed it, preventing Bek from mass producing Ambulas proxies capable of self-learning and adapting to combat situations.
