## Lore & History

Quincy served with Arthur in the Crimson Watch as a means to obtain the funds to attend college, and thus uplift his family. Whilst on overwatch during a mission, Quincy attempted to abandon his position, and was caught by enemy soldiers. Arthur, however, rescued him before he was executed. Despite his anger towards Arthur for, in his eyes, forcing Quincy to care about what happens to him, he feels he owes Arthur a debt. Quincy ultimately leaves the Crimson Watch with Arthur when he is honorably discharged, and follows him into the ICR.

### Protoframe Experiments

Main article: Fragments/Albrecht

Quincy was one of the first volunteers that came forward to accept Dr. Entrati's offer to receive more than healing. He and the others were then infused with a Helminth serum from Albrecht's time, which in turn morphed and alchemized them into partial Warframes. Their bodies became newly attuned to the Void, whilst their minds were kept intact.

Repeated samples were taken from the now-transformed volunteers by Albrecht. It was implied by the doctor himself that their humanity may not last, and that it may be only a matter of time until the Helminth finally swallows them whole.

### The Hex

In Höllvania Central Mall, Quincy is at the shooting range when he is approached by Arthur for his Atomicycle keys, but Quincy brushes him off. When Arthur attempts to ride out, Quincy and the rest of The Hex show up and refuse to allow Arthur to investigate the lead on Dr. Entrati on his own. Arthur instructs Quincy, Amir, and Aoi to provide backup while Leticia and Eleanor hold the fort.

When Arthur bring backs the Drifter, who warns them that Höllvania's nuclear reactor will explode at the end of 1999, the Hex don't buy it - but Arthur has them take point to search for Dr. Entrati. Quincy hands the Drifter a few loaner weapons.

After much searching, the Hex and the Drifter finally find Entrati, but Entrati and the Drifter are then taken away by the Indifference.

The Hex decide to stop the nuclear explosion themselves, but Quincy decides to bail. He later returns after an implied change of heart, jokingly telling Arthur that he is "proper shit at directions" as he provides covering fire for Arthur, Amir, and Aoi. However, the Hex ultimately fail to stop the explosion, with Quincy killed by a H-04 Efervon Tank. The Drifter resolves to save all of the Hex and rewinds time to the beginning of 1999, following a cryptic warning from Entrati to befriend the Hex.

### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Quincy

Quincy's username is "Soldja1Shot1kil". He is outgoing, confident, and a flirt, with a deeper connection to the city and its morally grey underground than the other members of the Hex, often bartering with civilians for contraband and other goods. He views the world as a big conglomeration of transactional relationships, and will constantly offer services to the Drifter, in exchange for a service for himself - he particularly hates being in debt to someone.

Quincy takes issue with how Arthur still takes himself too seriously, claiming that he needs to lighten up, as their situation does not entirely merit the military doctrine that Arthur tries to follow. He also holds a mild grudge against Arthur for having saved his life, as, in his worldview, he now owes Arthur.

Quincy seems to have a strong sense of independence, as he runs a side hustle with Höllvanian citizens for cosmetic goods despite Arthur's orders and preferences, and is irritated by the idea that the Drifter might see the Hex as disposable "rides" given their ability to swap between various Warframes at will.

Quincy also likes to read educational books, or, if he is on sustained observation missions, listen to audiobooks, covering subjects such as finance, statistics, science, and physics. He was once pranked by his squad when they replaced the label on one of his audiobooks to trick him into bringing along a "raunchy romance book" - he ended up enjoying the book for the plot rather than the explicit sections.

It is also shown in the chats that he enjoys photography as he likens getting a good photo to aiming down the sights of a sniper rifle. He will mention that his father died when he was young and found his old camera, inspiring him to take photography as a hobby. The Drifter can flirt with him in this manner asking him to take a photo of them, with clothing being optional.

When asked about his favorite music, Quincy mentions the time back in their ICR days that he and the others were at a karaoke bar, and Arthur sang an English folk song called 'The Unquiet Grave'; whose origins are uncertain, dating back to the 15th/16th Century. The lyrics gave Quincy such chills that he remembers them word-for-word, even in the present day.

Despite his bickering with Amir, Quincy worries that Amir, having had less experience with war and combat, may end up being much more strongly affected by his actions than the rest of the Hex. The Drifter has the option to advise Quincy to share his war-related experiences with Amir to smooth out their relationship. While he ostensibly dislikes "dorky" hobbies like Fables and Frontiers, he mentions that Arthur would definitely be a Paladin, before correcting himself and saying that he would not know what a paladin is. If convinced to play, Amir willl muse in later conversations that he can see Quincy enjoying the experience despite not being willing to admit it at first.

According to Amir, Quincy would engage quite loudly in sex in the mall, preventing him from sleeping. The Drifter has the option to advise Amir to ask Quincy to stop, and Quincy is later noted to have been "mortified" by the request while agreeing to do his sexual activities elsewhere.

One particular service Quincy offers is romantic advice for the rest of the members of the Hex. At higher Chemistry ranks, he will assist Drifter to romance Hex members by having the target person initiate conversation. If a breakup or rejection occurred, Quincy can also convince the target person, including Quincy himself, to give the Drifter a second chance.

Once the relationship with Quincy has reached "Close", he will call all the favors the Drifter owes him and will ask the worst thing the Drifter ever did so he can hate them, with the Drifter immediately getting angry about Quincy asking about their trauma as an exchange. Most of their choices during this particular chat can have the Drifter comply and reveal themselves feeling responsible for their parent's death, as well has having severe survivor's guilt because of it. However, depending on the Drifter's choices on previous chats, the Drifter can opt to call him out on his lies instead, stating that Quincy is too afraid to form genuine connections with anyone (as he seems to believe connections make him vulnerable), which is why he prefers to stay alone since it's safer for him. It's also shown he compares having connections with anyone to be in debt to that person. The Drifter can comply with his demands regardless after this particular route or refuse to and simply end the chat telling Quincy to grow up.

If one of the two aforementioned routes is taken, Quincy will reveal his background the next day and how being saved by Arthur led him to his current situation, leaving him with a very jaded view of the world in the process and making the Drifter uncomfortable was his way of making sure they knew how he felt about it. He will then ask the Drifter for forgiveness and will state that their relationship (romantic or not) will be considered true from that point. If the Drifter decides to forgive him, they will continue their conversations on the chat. However, if the Drifter refuses to forgive him, outright refuses to continue this particular chat, calls him a disappointment at the end of it or refuses to comply with his demands on the previous conversation from the beginning, Quincy will cease further contact over KIM and also break up with the Drifter if they are dating.

## Trivia

- Quincy and Arthur are former members of the Britannic Army.
- When loaning the Drifter weapons right after meeting them, Quincy refers to them as "Entrati's Cybercop" and "V-1000". These are references to the 1987 film RoboCop and the 1991 film Terminator 2: Judgment Day, with the former referencing the titular protagonist and the latter referencing the main antagonist, the T-1000.
- His overall appearance differs the most from his warframe template in comparison to the rest of the Hex — in particular, his left forearm has large hollows revealing a thin core inside, suggesting it is actually a prosthetic.
- Quincy often speaks and types using Jamaican Patois. It is revealed on KIM that his origin country prior to moving to Britannia is Xaymaca, which seems to be the the Warframe equivalent of Jamaica.
- Quincy also uses London slang — such as “fam” and “mandem” — associated with the “chav” and, more specifically, “roadman” subcultures. Chavs and roadmen are seen to dress flashy, be antisocial and speak in this manner, which line up with his antisocial behaviour towards Arthur and the Hex at times, and his KIM messages about the drifter's clothing.

Arthur mentions that Quincy would not be allowed to play Hide-and-Seek with some of the members of the Hex, as the latter would cheat using his scope to see through walls.

  - This is also one of the reasons why the Fables and Frontiers game would be later played by KIM rather than in person, as Quincy used his scope to spy Amir's computer.
- Quincy believes his body resembles a toaster oven-human hybrid. A toaster oven is also available as a gift.
- Even though his squad pranked him with a romance audio book during a sustained watch long before being turned into a protoframe, Quincy admits he actually grew to enjoy the genre, and has currently ran out. That said, he feels quite embarrassed when Minerva suggests trading her own romance audiobooks with him.
- One chat reveals he used to have nightmares after having to kill for the first time as a sniper. He shares this with Amir when talking to the latter about his own nightmares, if suggested to do so by The Drifter. Whether he still has these nightmares is unknown.

Quincy's initial death resembles the death of Daniel Jackson from the 1998 film Saving Private Ryan, where after killing several enemies in a sniper's perch, a tank fires on his position, killing him.

  - This is revealed to be a consequence of not fully knowing how to use his Warframe abilities until the Drifter guides him to use Seek in The Finale, as stated in later KIM conversations.
  - This is also shown to be the case for Aoi and Amir (Aoi being pinned down by a car when trying to launch it, and Amir not knowing that he had a Parazon to hack the nuclear reactor with).
