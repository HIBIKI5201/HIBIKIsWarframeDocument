#### Lore & History

##### Early life

Formerly named Euleria Entrati, she was born to the Orokin Entrati family to her parents Albrecht and Gerivaine, alongside many siblings. Euleria would inherit her mother's eyes. Gerivaine, however, would pass away early on, leaving Albrecht to raise Euleria all by himself inconsistently.

During Euleria's childhood, Albrecht would come up with a certain cast of characters and tell her stories for her entertainment.

##### The First Void Expedition

Main article: Albrecht Entrati

During the first voyage into the Void, Mother was the one to start the experiment, opening the Wall of Lohk for her father to journey into with the use of a Seriglass Bell. Albrecht would ultimately be successful, but not without consequence. As he tumbled back out of the Wall, he would command Mother to close and deactivate the gate in order to prevent an unknown eldritch entity from fully emerging. However, when closing the Wall, she would inadvertently sever some of the entity's fingers.

##### Zariman Ten-Zero

With the severed digits, both Albrecht and Euleria would both study all they could of the Void, and would aid the Orokin in constructing the Zariman Ten Zero; a large colonist ship that was intended to travel and colonize the Tau System. Additionally, Euleria herself would provide instructional presentations to the children aboard the Zariman, with some notable subjects being Temporal Axioms and the Theories of Time; Presentism and Eternalism.

Euleria would later write "The Palimpsest of Spacetime" alongside her father. Additionally, she would author the storybook, "Tales of Duviri", based on the stories that her father told in her childhood. Set in the kingdom of Duviri, the book itself was created to teach children emotional control and regulation in the event of Void exposure. The story focuses on child-king, Dominus Thrax, and his five courtiers, who each represent one of his emotions. The Drifter, a survivor aboard the Zariman Ten Zero, would inadvertently recreate Duviri and its tales within the Void as a result of their trauma, which they had since long forgotten while trying to escape it.

Sometime after the Zariman was constructed, Euleria's father would mysteriously disappear, prompting her to begin her search in finding him. However, this deed would soon turn into obsession.

Around this time, Euleria would be courted by Quinn, although Quinn's efforts never went anywhere, as Euleria later chose to marry Petros Vorten instead. Recognizing Euleria's low morale amidst her father's disappearance, Petros would try to console her.

##### The Infestation of Deimos

Main article: Citrine

After a bio-bomb was detonated in Deimos in an attempt to repel the Sentients, the entire moon was overrun with Infested. The Entrati had no choice but to lock themselves in the Necralisk and use it as a makeshift safehouse. After the Collapse of the Orokin Empire, there were no Dax or Tenno left to assist in the protection of moon, which resulted in its gradual decline and the abandonment of the Entrati's subterranean Isolation Vaults, after the Grey Strain began to encroach upon the Cambion Drift. Due to Son's habit of interacting with the Infested animals, he would inadvertently break containment protocol and allow Infested spores to make their way inside the Necralisk. With the entire Entrati family infected, Euleria lost her name to the Infested hivemind and became known as Mother.

Mother's relationship with the rest of the family was highly emotionally abusive. In general, she did not seem to value her marriage, nor pay much attention to her husband and children, often disregarding or belittling them. Additionally, she often believed she could hear her father Albrecht tapping from beyond the Wall, and with only a shard of his Seriglass Bell, Mother would continuously attempt to look into the Void to find him, even as the Necralisk succumbed to the Infestation. She would often order the rest of the family to help her with her search, and became increasingly paranoid as time went on, even believing that they were conspiring against her.

Her husband, Father, would attempt to console her, telling her to put the shard away and move on. However, Mother angrily attacked him, repeatedly cutting and lacerating him with the shard while the children looked on. Due to the previous contamination of the Necralisk, Mother would soon become infected, along with the rest of the family.

As punishment, Mother collared Son, stripping him of his command words and banishing him from the family archives. The Entrati would then slowly start succumbing to the Infestation, losing many of their memories.

##### The Re-emergence of Deimos

Main article: Heart of Deimos

Recently, Mother noticed the Heart was beginning to break down. Her husband is the only one with the expertise to repair it, however he was nowhere to be found, so with the assistance of a the family's loyal Necraloid servant, Loid, she sends out a distress call for any available Tenno to come and aid in its repair.

When the Tenno arrive, Mother is first to greet them. #### Lore & History

##### General

Formerly known as Petros Vorten, Father belongs to the Entrati family, who were highly regarded since the Orokin Era for being the first ones to create technologies that harnessed the power of the Void. He, along with his wife, Mother, are the custodians of the Heart; a device made by her father that is said to "pump the arteries" of the known universe and the Void, and serves as the wellspring of Void power for the Tenno.

##### The Old War

It was revealed that Father had spent some time in front-line combat during the Old War as a Railjack gunner with a crew of three others; Barroc, Kailen, and Mutaru. While patching ruptures in the Delta Proxima, he mentioned how unsettling it was for them to see a Sentient Murex for the first time.

Sometime later, Father would then meet Mother (known at that time as Euleria Entrati), who was continuing to search for her missing father, Albrecht Entrati. He would then marry her, believing that he could help her move past the loss of her father.

To fight against the invading Sentient forces, Father would go on to design large, bipedal war machines known as Necramechs, including one modeled after his wife. With their Void-shielding, the Necramechs were a solid defense against the Sentients. However, due to their rudimentary behavior, the Orokin chose to retire the mechs early in favor of the more advanced Warframes.

##### The Infestation of Deimos

Main article: Citrine

After a bio-bomb was detonated in Deimos in an attempt to repel the Sentients, the entire moon was overrun with Infested. The Entrati had no choice but to lock themselves in the Necralisk and use it as a makeshift safehouse. After the Collapse of the Orokin Empire, there were no Dax or Tenno left to assist in the protection of moon, which resulted in its gradual decline and the abandonment of the Entrati's subterranean Isolation Vaults, after the Grey Strain began to encroach upon the Cambion Drift.

Due to Son's habit of interacting with the Infested animals, he would inadvertently break containment protocol and allow Infested spores to make their way inside the Necralisk. One day, he would dress up a pair of Infested creatures as his parents, giving them names of honor as a joke. Father, seeing this as a mockery and an insult, promptly slaughtered the creatures as per containment protocol.

Father's marriage with his wife was heavily strained, as Mother only seemed to value her father's seriglass shard more than their marriage. Additonally, he would belittle his daughter's fish collection and refuse to listen to his son's theories about the Infested wildlife. Every morning, Father would attempt to lead the children in martial discipline exercises. However, his daughter would become increasingly sick of them, and would eventually lash out, clawing and severing her father's left arm with the claw he gave her.

He would attempt to console his wife about her missing father, attempting to convince her to put her seriglass shard away and move on. Mother refused, and began to angrily attack him, cutting and scratching him with the shard while the children looked on. Instead of fighting back, Father simply stood there, allowing every strike to hit him.

Due to the previous contamination of the Necralisk, Father would soon become infected, along with the rest of the family. They were able to cling to their identities, even as their memories began to dim. Father took advantage of the confusion to flee from his abusive wife, and chose to reside within the Cambion Drift to watch his rogue Necramechs duel with the Infested.

##### The Re-emergence Of Deimos

Main article: Heart of Deimos

Due to the Heart currently failing, the Entrati has no choice but to re-phase Deimos back into Martian orbit. Mother tasks the Tenno to track down Father to help fix the Heart. Now estranged from the rest of his family, the Tenno find him out in the Cambion Drift, watching his Necramechs fight against the roaming Infested. However, he does not believe the Heart is actually failing, and that Mother is just trying to manipulate him into returning back home.

After a rogue Necramech disables the Heart, Father reluctantly returns to the Necralisk. Together, the family and the Tenno construct a plan to repair the Heart, with Father placing the Tenno into an Old-War-era Necramech dubbed Snake.

With the Heart restored, Father now reluctantly stays within the Necralisk, now intending to do things his way and show the rest of the family what he is capable of. He seeks to arm the Tenno for their excursions into the Cambion Drift, requiring a constant supply of Infested resources to build weapons and firearms. Additonally, Father collaborates with Loid to provide them with Necramech parts in secret.

Son exhibits a small desire to let the Infestation win, stating how it might not be the 'abomination' the Tenno thinks it is. Fortunately, Son is able to put his squabbles aside, and provides assistance to his family and the Tenno in repairing the Heart.

Unlike the rest of his family, Son is fully accepting of his Infested nature. He now seeks to understand the moon's unique processes and function by studying its wildlife, and will reward the Tenno if they bring him intact samples and specimens.

#### Lore & History

##### General

Daughter belongs to the Entrati family, who were highly regarded since the Orokin Era for being the first ones to create technologies that harnessed the power of the Void.

As a biologist and researcher, Daughter specializes in xenobiology and xenobotany, with a special focus in synergetic Void harmonics. Additionally, she states that she was disqualified from applying to the Archimedean Collegium "on a technicality".

##### The Infestation of Deimos

After the Collapse of the Orokin Empire, there were no Dax or Tenno left to assist in the protection of Deimos, which resulted in the gradual decline of the Entrati family and abandonment of their subterranean Isolation Vaults, after the encroaching Infested Grey Strain started to increase.

Daughter was not originally scheduled to accompany her family to Deimos, but stowed away to join them nonetheless. On Deimos she was tasked with genetic engineering and experimentation on several genera of fish. Trapped in the Necralisk with her neglectful parents and an envious brother, things became strained pretty quickly. Eventually her brother stole her fish, leaving behind a box of ash to make her believe he'd killed them, and broke the containment protocols to let the Infested in. The Entrati became infested themselves in short order, slowly losing their memories though not their identities.

##### The Re-emergence of Deimos

Main article: Heart of Deimos

Recently, Daughter has been engrossed in her research into the nature of the Infestation and its hive mind. She tasks the Tenno with bringing her samples of Infested fish for study, as she fears that they will all eventually surrender their individuality to the encroaching Infestation and be assimilated. Through her observations of the giant infested wyrms, Fass and Vome, and their fighting amongst themselves which determines the time of day on Deimos, as well as her observations on the Infested fish of the Cambion Drift, Daughter hopes to find a way to preserve her family's independent consciousnesses and wills before they fully succumb.

Along with Loid, Daughter is also tasked with securing nutrition and preparing simple food that the rest of the family can ingest in their Infested state, such as stew made from Infested body parts. However, Daughter instead has gained a taste Fungal Polyps; Infested masses of fungus that, when fermented in the digestive system of a Juggernaut, give psychedelic dreams when consumed, and tasks and nearby Tenno to forage for them, despite Mother's disagreements.

#### Lore & History

##### General

Grandmother belongs to the Entrati family, who were highly regarded since the Orokin Era for being the first ones to create technologies that harnessed the power of the Void. Her original name was Dagath, but during one Naberus celebration she gave it to a then-nameless and faceless Warframe, becoming known as Grandmother forevermore.

Grandmother herself is the mother of Father.

##### The Infestation of Deimos

Main article: Citrine

After a bio-bomb was detonated in Deimos in an attempt to repel the Sentients, the entire moon was overrun with Infested. The Entrati had no choice but to lock themselves in the Necralisk and use it as a makeshift safehouse. After the Collapse of the Orokin Empire, there were no Dax or Tenno left to assist in the protection of moon, which resulted in its gradual decline and the abandonment of the Entrati's subterranean Isolation Vaults, after the Grey Strain began to encroach upon the Cambion Drift. Due to Son's habit of interacting with the Infested animals, he would inadvertently break containment protocol and allow Infested spores to make their way inside the Necralisk.

How the Orokin known as Grandmother came to the Deimos expedition is not specified; her grandchildren state she was not a soldier nor a scientist. Nonetheless, she was soon trapped in the Necralisk with her son, her daughter-in-law, and her two grandchildren. While the rest of the family bickered and argued, Grandmother would instead act as a neutral party, often earning their ire. She believed her son's martial discipline exercises were futile attempts at keeping order, however she would not approve of her granddaughter's ridicule, seeing it as too excessive.

Grandmother would attempt to heal the family and keep them together, often taking care of their wounds or ailments. Despite her best efforts, the family became increasingly dysfunctional.

Due to the previous contamination of the Necralisk, Grandmother would soon become infected, along with the rest of the family. The Entrati would then slowly start succumbing to the Infestation, losing many of their memories, though not their identities.

##### The Re-emergence of Deimos

Main article: Heart of Deimos

After the Tenno reunite the Entrati family to restore a destroyed Heart, they meet Grandmother,

However, she comments that, despite their dysfunctional nature, the family will come together in a crisis. The Tenno glean from the conversation that Grandmother caused the events surrounding the Heart's destruction to unite the family.

##### The Waverider

Grandmother, having discovered a Graphica comic dubbed "Waverider #1" in Nef Anyo's possession, offered to purchase the completed Graphica from him. He tasks Roky to unlock the Graphica, but she is gravely injured in the process, prompting Boon and the Tenno to take action in her stead. Nef slips having a buyer on Deimos (who is in fact, Grandmother), allowing Boon to contact the buyer and make a deal.

Once the Graphica is completed, Nef Anyo discovers the buyer had backed out, thus leaving him with an unsellable comic he deems useless, instead buying out of the ventilation shafts where the Ventkids live. Boon reveals that he secretly sold a copy of the Graphica to the buyer in exchange for an elixir to heal Roky and removal of their debts. Grandmother, now the Ventkids' new landlady, expresses to the Tenno her disappointment at Nef's greed and his dismissal of what he deems 'commoner art', revealing the completed Graphica of Waverider #1 contains a blueprint for the Yareli Warframe.

#### Appearance

Kalymos is quite unique-looking compared to other Kavat breeds. She is completely hairless except for her whiskers, leaving her pale green skin bare. Her eyes uniquely glow solid blue, without distinction of sclera and pupil, similar to the rest of House Entrati's members. She also has a single tail with a fan-shaped tip.

Kalymos wears golden ornaments on her shoulders, and bracelets on her limbs and tail. Her ears are adorned with golden curved earpieces. She wears a long collar adorned with Entrati Obols.

#### Lore & History

##### General

Unlike with other animals, Albrecht didn’t send Kalymos into Void expeditions due to sentimental values and her intelligence, although he does mention it would’ve been more ethical to use her as opposed to the last pair of Cervulites.

According to Loid, Kalymos was once very sick and was kept in seclusion for some time, but later mysteriously returned healthy. Loid speculates that Kalymos may have been cloned.

##### Whispers in the Walls

"The Kalymos Sequence" is named after the Kavat.

Kalymos traveled to the year of 1999 alongside her master.

##### The Lotus Eaters

When the Lotus instructs the Drifter to answer the call on the ringing pager in Sanctum Anatomica, they are suddenly warped to an abandoned mall. The Drifter then encounters Kalymos, who leads them around the abandoned mall, eventually leading them to another ringing pager before disappearing.

##### The Hex

Kalymos is first encountered by Arthur alongside Albrecht Entrati who then vanishes. She later appears in the Höllvania Central Mall to guide the Drifter to the Backroom where they receive a message from her master.

After the quest, Kalymos continues to hang around the Central Mall, though some of the Hex (especially Leticia, fearing Kalymos may go after her rats) remain wary of her. She also provides assistance in hunting the Legacyte for Legacyte Harvest.

#### Trivia

- The name Kalymos is likely derived from the name Kalydnos, one of the titans in Greek mythology. Furthermore, Kalymos' traits of resilience and fortitude derive the mythos associated with this titan, one who clung onto an island to save himself from falling into Tartarus. Alternatively, Kalymos may have been named after the Greek island of Kalymnos.
- The general concept of Kalymos appears to derive from Bubastis, the faithful evolved cat of Watchmen's morally compromised anti-villain, Ozymandias.

A Kalymos-themed Kavat Gene Masking Kit can be purchased from the Market for 55 and included in Sanctum Supporter Pack.

  - While Kalymos is a female kavat, the kavat in Sanctum Supporter Pack is a male Vasca Kavat.
- In Amir's New Years Kiss cutscene, he throws his glasses away, causing a cat's yowling.
- If the player is not in a relationship with any of the Hex members but is Best Friends with all six of them by New Years, they will get a unique inbox message from Kalymos (though it is actually signed by Arthur) and a cutscene featuring the Drifter sharing a hug with Kalymos during New Years.

#### Lore & History

##### General

Loid is a Necraloid, an Orokin Cephalon created by Albrecht Entrati in order to serve his family. Unlike most Cephalons, who are digital constructs without a physical form, Loid is installed within a floating Sentinel-like shell.

Some time after the infestation of Deimos, Loid attempted to assist Otak, another Cephalon in service to the Entrati, by uploading what remained of Otak's personality into his own shell. However, part of the defragmentation process failed, leaving Otak irreparably damaged. While the two Cephalons are mostly capable of switching between each other, Otak occasionally interrupts Loid with erratic, whimsical remarks.

##### The Infestation of Deimos

##### Transcripts of Unauthorized Broadcasts

In the lead-up to TennoCon 2023, Nora Night, during a slow and uneventful night, finally decides to open her own call line, conversing with three unknown callers over the course of three days.

Transmission Origin: [CRITICAL ERROR] / Time Since Intercept - 9 Hours The third caller is Loid, attempting to remain anonymous by using the alias "Bones". He states that something not intended to happen to his kind has occurred, and that he feels compelled to share it, but doesn't know who to share it with. Loid then reveals that his partner, presumably Otak, has abruptly gone missing, and that he is now alone. Nora attempts to console Loid, asking if there is anyone he can ask for help. Loid then realizes that they both know someone capable of dealing with the unknown, and states that they must be summoned immediately.

##### Whispers in the Walls

As the threat of The Indifference grows, Loid becomes aware of a previously-hidden precept known as the Kalymos Sequence. He asks the Tenno to meet him in the Necralisk and leads them beneath it into the Sanctum Anatomica and Albrecht's Laboratories. There they fulfill the first step of the Sequence by awakening 'the Sleeper' - revealed to be the original, human Loid, who the Necraloid was created in order to accompany and assist. However, the human Loid shows disdain towards his Cephalon replica and opts to send him back up to the Necralisk to ensure the rest of the family remains asleep and unaware of the laboratories.

After completing the quest, Necraloid can ask about the progress of the Kalymos Sequence if spoken to in the Necralisk. He additionally implies he had forced Otak into slumber to keep him away from the labs, believing that the truth of the situation might harm him. It is unclear whether this is a separate incident to the disappearance he had earlier discussed with Nora Night, or if the two are the same and he opted to lie about the situation.

##### Deep Archimedea

After Loid and the Cavia settled into the laboratories, Necraloid began splitting his time between his duties in the Necralisk and helping in the Sanctum Anatomica by running Deep Archimedea, a set of volatile experiments with strict parameters. Failure of said experiments would risk the Entrati family becoming aware of the existence of Albrecht's Laboratories, so Loid tasked his Necraloid self with ensuring all went smoothly.

#### Trivia

- Loid is the only NPC in Necralisk who doesn't have his own Entrati Family Token, presumably due to him being his own syndicate instead of a member of the Entrati syndicate.
- Loid (and Otak) are the first NPC to have a companion skin, the second of which being Ordis.
- After the original Loid's awakening in Whispers in the Walls, Loid is referred to in subtitles as "Necraloid" for the remainder of the quest to prevent confusion. This does not apply to his appearances in the Necralisk or the Cambion Drift.

While discussing Dante, the original Loid mentions having had his skull replicated. As all other known instances of Cephalon creation have involved the death of the subject, it is almost certainly this replica from which Necraloid was created.

  - This is further supported in a KIM chat with Marie wherein she recounts an exchange involving Loid and Necraloid, with Necraloid referring to 'his' skull vs. the 'original', while Loid refers to Necraloid as a replica.
  - This implies that Necraloid is a Cephalon copy of Loid— a construct born from Loid's cloned mind. Apparently, creating Cephalons this way was always possible, but the Orokin insisted on glassing the original person, an insistence Albrecht and Loid had no respect of. Presumably Otak is a true Cephalon, however.

#### Lore & History

Loid was present when Albrecht Entrati travelled back to the year of 1999, and was given the responsibility of breaking the device used afterwards so that he could not be followed. It is unclear whether Loid was still serving the Entrati family as himself at this time or whether the Necraloid version of himself had already been created; either way, following this event, Loid was placed into cryostasis as part of the procedure of the Kalymos Sequence. The family were left unaware of his survival, believing that he had died and been turned into a Necraloid.

##### Whispers in the Walls

Due to the increased threat of The Indifference, the Necraloid awakens the Kalymos Sequence and leads the Tenno down to Albrecht's Laboratories and Sanctum Anatomica, where they battle The Murmur and awaken the Sleeper - the human, original Loid - from cryostasis, revealing that the Necraloid was created to accompany and assist his human counterpart. However, the original Loid refers to the Necraloid with disdain and opts to send him back up to the Necralisk to ensure the rest of the family remains asleep and unaware of the laboratories.

Loid reveals that the Kalymos Sequence requires the Operator to use Transference on Albrecht's Vessels. However, upon doing so they find themselves within 1999, revealing that the Indifference had successfully followed Albrecht back in-time. Meanwhile in the present, the Indifference attempts to taunt Loid.

A message from Albrecht is extracted from the Transference buffer, explaining that his efforts to time travel were done to protect Loid from the Indifference, and also leaves behind a blueprint for the Jahu Gargoyle that translates the Cavia's Voidtongue. The now-capable speaking Fibonacci reveals that Loid tossed away a message for him from Albrecht's Grimoire, unwilling to read his self-justifications. The Sanctum is then under attack by The Fragmented which is made vulnerable to the contents of the recovered page: Albrecht's love and praise for Loid's servitude. A Void-controlled Vessel attempts to attack, but is placated and pacified by a Tenno-controlled Vessel.

Loid, witnessing this act of love he had long since craved from Albrecht, is determined to see things through to the end and makes preparations to send the Tenno to 1999.

##### The Lotus Eaters

With the help of the Lotus, Loid discovers a connection between the Vessels and the Protoframes and is fully prepared to send the Drifter back to 1999.

##### The Old Peace

Loid assists with the working of the Dark Refractory and monitors the security systems of the labs while the Lotus and the Operator dive into the latter's lost memories of Tau. When they resurface abruptly he expresses reservations about the process, encouraging the Operator to stop digging for fear of what "polluted histories" they might be left with. However, when the Operator tells him about their friend he relents and allows them to continue diving.

As The Murmur continue to assault the labs to the point of disrupting the Jahu Gargoyle's functionality and reverting the Cavia's ability of speech, he attempts again to convince the Operator to stop, reminding them when they become distressed that the events they are undergoing are only memories and the outcome cannot be changed.

The Murmur eventually succeed in breaking the lab's defenses, although the Operator is able to repel them. Loid firmly states that this will be the final time he allows them into the Dark Refractory until they have adequately rested, as the system greatly taxes the user.

##### The Devil's Triad

Loid contacts the Drifter over Kinemantik Instant Messenger informing them that three new Protoframes have suddenly appeared in Sanctum Anatomica: Lyon, Marie, and Roathe.

Roathe will eventually reveal his plan to have Loid reveal himself and everything that has been happening beneath the Necralisk to the Entrati, requesting The Drifter's assistance to do it. If the Drifter is able to enlist Marie's and Lyon's help to convince Loid along with Roathe, the latter will finally make the decision to inform the family, though he fears Gomaitru and the rest will tear his limbs in a fit of a rage upon their discovery of the things happening beneath their feet and asks The Drifter for support when the time comes.

#### General

Fibonacci offers Sanctum Anatomica Bounties, which reward an item and Cavia Standing. He also allows the player to rank up with the Cavia.

Reaching Rank 5 with the Cavia unlocks additional options to customize Fibonacci's colors and offer him comfort.

#### Lore & History

##### Albrecht's Notes

Fibonacci, Bird 3, Tagfer and Minn were the Cavia of "Mission 3-1", the latest of several attempts by Albrecht Entrati to have The Indifference copy a more primitive mind like it had copied a human mind. Fibonacci was chosen for being a fish of high mental capacity for an animal, but was the only one who was unnamed. As the Cavia were sent to the Void, The Indifference breached in and gave them human-level intelligence and sapience, also teaching them its Voidtongue. Fibonacci panicked at that moment and opened the seal to the Seriglass bell, accidentally ejecting Minn and giving the Cavia a large dosage of Void exposure. When they returned from the Void, Albrecht decided not to dispose of the Cavia, in hopes that someone would eventually be able to understand the Voidtongue they spoke, and assigned them to the care of Loid instead. Loid chose to freeze the Cavia in hopes that a cure for Void exposure could be found.

##### Whispers in the Walls

After Loid awakens, the Cavia awaken as well, speaking Voidtongue to the Tenno. Later, they retrieve and build the blueprint for the Jahu Gargoyle, allowing the Cavia to speak in an intelligible language. Fibonacci reveals that they are part of the Kalymos Sequence, and instructs the Tenno to find the final page of the Grimoire. The completed Grimoire is given to the Jahu Gargoyle, allowing The Indifference to be repelled from the Sanctum Anatomica.

##### Cavia

Fibonacci tries his best to act as the leader of the Cavia, but he frictions with Tagfer's resentment of him for being responsible for Minn's death. This reaches the point where Tagfer tries to break Fibonacci's tank, but is stopped by Loid. Tagfer later concedes that he can work with Fibonacci to contain The Murmur and find Albrecht Entrati. However, the Cavia become increasingly ill due to their prior Void exposure.

Loid prepares a chamber to deal with the Void sickness, but the process appears to fail. The Cavia die, then their Void contamination levels alchemize, bringing them back to life, while also giving them the ability to change colors.

#### Overview

Unlike his contemporaries, Bird 3 seems to have has a simplistic, naive point of view like that of a child, being unable to process Tagfer's grief or Fibonacci's intellectualism while he asks endless questions about topics others would consider childish or simple. This does not mean he is unintelligent however; with his aggressive curiosity sometimes resulting in comments of surprising insight borne from an unconventional viewpoint. Bird 3 is often the one of the Cavia trio to recognize when the other two are getting bogged down in unnecessary bickering.

#### Lore & History

Bird 3, alongside who would become Fibonacci and Tagfer, is a survivor of the last batch of Cavia Albrecht Entrati used as his first attempts to fight The Indifference, and was loaded alongside Fibonacci as expendable "mental ballast" compared to the uniqueness of Tagfer and Minn. As with the rest of the Cavia, he gained sentience and understanding of the Voidtongue after the experiment, but was left unable to speak in any other language, on top of being exposed to lethal amounts of Void contamination. Albrecht, initially assuming the experiment was yet another failure, ultimately spared their lives, believing that someone more accustomed to the Void would understand them. He entrusted their care to Loid, who preserved the Cavia in cryogenic sleep which allowed them to survive to the present day.

With the growing strength of The Indifference, Loid would resuscitate the Cavia as the Tenno searched Albrecht's Laboratories for information regarding the then-recently activated Kaylmos Sequence. Bird 3, with his simplistic mindset, would be put in charge of selling the Cavia's wares to the Tenno and managing straightforward missions against The Murmur. While seemingly uninvolved with Tagfer's growing hostilities against Fibonacci (in regards to the latter killing Minn by accident as the Cavia encountered The Indifference, which also led to them getting lethally exposed to the Void), Bird 3 loses his patience and points out that Tagfer is not the only one affected by Albrecht's actions, and that all the Cavia have left at this point is each other.

The Cavia would succumb to their Void exposure, though would revive themselves shortly afterwards (presumably through Conceptual Embodiment). Of The Cavia, Bird 3 was delighted at his newfound ability to change his appearance.

#### Trivia

While he does not appear in person, Höllvania has a zoo display depicting a bird species similar to Bird 3, presumably an ancestor species during that time.

  - The display names the bird the "Wuk", a species native to South America, where it predominantly lives in the Amazon Rainforest, and has an aggresive temperament. Compared to Bird 3, this species has a more "upright" posture, longer legs, more developed wings, and different tail feathers.
- After arriving at the Sanctum, Necraloid Loid occasionally mentions the existence of a "Bird 1" and "Bird 2", but requests the Tenno not to ask their whereabouts.

#### Trivia

- Tagfer's concept art was first revealed at TennoCon 2019 under the name "Eidolon Critter".
- Tagfer's species name, Cervulite, may be derived from the word cervine, describing deer-like creatures.

Tagfer and Minn were "tagged for disposal", intended to be discarded by Albrecht an attempt to sacrifice the last Cervulites to The Indifference to satisfy its hunger for uniqueness. After Minn's passing and Tagfer's gained sentience, he chose to use this fact to name himself based on this categorization. ("tag fer" disposal)

  - An alternative origin to Tagfer's name can be found in Höllvania's Zoological Gardens, which show an ancient species of horned ungulate that might be an ancestor to the Cervulite. The species' name is written as TGFR, "Tagfer", in 1999's proto-Orokin script. Those ungulates were native to Canata, Warframe's equivalent to Canada.
