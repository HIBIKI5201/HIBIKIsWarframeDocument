## 概要

Deimos に派遣された Grineer の兵士

> Wily Grineer castaway, acclaimed as squad leader by his surviving brood-mates. Capable of complex diplomatic negotiations by Grineer standards.
> Garv is a unique Grineer trooper deployed to Deimos in need of assistance. He is accompanied by a friendly Trooper Survivor and Lancer Survivor.
> Garv and his squad can appear in Cambion Drift Bounties, where Tenno must protect him from the Infested. However, he issues a challenge to see whether the Grineer or the Tenno squads can kill the most Infested, awarding a bonus if the Tenno's kill count is double than that of the Grineer's.

## Trivia

- According to the official drop tables, Garv has a 1% chance to drop a Gorgon Blueprint if he dies. This may be due to the fact that he reuses the Heavy Gunner code back end, who all have a 1% chance of dropping a Gorgon Blueprint. This inadvertently caused a bug prior to Hotfix 29.0.7 (2020-09-03) where when giving Garv triple armor values, all Heavy Gunner enemies received triple armor values.
- After added to the Codex with scanning, Garv can be summoned in the Simulacrum.
- Garv is immune to critical hits.
