## 概要

Zariman の事故直後に最期を記録した生存者

> “These last few weeks have been rough, but... we just need to hold out a little longer. One jump and we'll be in paradise, right? Okay! I'll be home late. Love you, my angel.”
> —The Husband
> The Husband is the alias for an unnamed survivor of the early events during Void-Jump Incident who recorded his final moments on Zariman logs. The logs were recovered during the Zariman ARG by a Void manifestation of Kira, his wife gone insane from void exposure and killed at the hands of a Tenno. Due to the events of the Zariman ARG, Kira became the Void Angel seen in the Angels of the Zariman quest.
