## 概要

Inversion Tree の幻視で狂い、亡くなった

> “Jinn. Five nights hung
> Upon the Inversion Tree What she saw Driven to recount Jinn. Five nights hung Upon the Inversion Tree Went to her end Chattering Recount not What was relayed ” —Jinn's tombstone epitaph
> Jinn was a resident of Duviri. Jinn was driven mad by visions from the Inversion Tree, which eventually led to her death.
> Her grave can be seen in Titan's Rest. You can read the epitaph by interacting with the tombstone.
