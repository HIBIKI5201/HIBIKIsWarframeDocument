## Lore

### The New War

During the events of the quest, Ka-Nuteru and her squad of Grineer were stationed in the sealabs of Uranus. They were hunting the Stalker, who was slowly thinning down the Grineer until only Ka-Nuteru was left. Despite her faith, the disciple is the last to fall, fearfully muttering "all as one" twice before being cut off.

Once the Drifter arrives at the sight of the scuffle, they can see the disciple's body impaled to the wall, killed by Stalker like the rest of her troops.

## Trivia

- While Ka-Nuteru's design implies she is either a Drekar Ballista or Heavy Gunner, she sports a unique black primary color with purple and white accents that do not match any of the Drekar units on Uranus.
