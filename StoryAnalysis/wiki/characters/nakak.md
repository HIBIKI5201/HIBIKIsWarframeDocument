## 概要

仮面屋の若い店主

> For her event shop, see Dog Days#Offerings.
> “Free advice with or without purchase, surah. I'm here to help!”
> —Nakak
> Nakak is a young saleswoman of "Masks and Oddities" in Cetus, located on the left side of the market, directly across from the gate to the Plains of Eidolon. She sells masks in the likeness of various Warframes and the Grineer, and Fosfors, scavenged Grineer flares that increase Affinity range when used. Instead of selling items for Standing or Platinum, Nakak trades her wares for resources from the Plains. Her stock occasionally changes while the costs associated with it change daily.

## Trivia

- In Thai, the word Nakak (หน้ากาก) translates to "mask."
- While Nakak herself does not sell rugs and carpets as claimed, the Ostron Rug Collection is available for platinum from the Market terminal aboard the Orbiter.

Nakak is currently the only child who can be seen in Cetus, outside the intro cutscene when first landing in Cetus. All of these children, including her, wear a Trinity mask.

  - Her face is revealed during the Mask of the Revenant mini-quest, and is the only child in Cetus to have their face revealed.
- During Operation: Plague Star, Nakak was also in charge of the event wares as the representative of the Operational Supply syndicate. She plays a similar role with the recurring Dog Days Tactical Alert, trading Nakak Pearls for event wares.

During the Mask of the Revenant mini-quest, it was revealed that Nakak has a cousin named Mukha, whose name is derived from Sanskrit, which translates to "face".

    - Unlike other NPCs who interact differently with Warframes whom they have special relationships with (such as Baro Ki'Teer with Inaros, Roky with Yareli and Quinn with Gyre), Nakak has no special interactions whenever she is visited by the Operator/Drifter in Revenant's form, possibly because she knows Mukha no longer inhabits the vessel.
- Nakak is voiced by Maya Tuttle.
