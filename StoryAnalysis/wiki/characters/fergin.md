> “'You ever do requests? You do? Go to the edge and jump!' That's what he'd say. Every day.”

> Fergin was a resident of Duviri. His komi table can be found on the edge of Lonesome Outlook.
> Fergin is the brother of Pip, a shawzinist in Duviri. He would often ridicule his brother's shawzin playing, stating that komi was superior. However, Fergin mysteriously went missing one day, and never came back.
