## General

Outfitted with a personal pair of gardening shears and hose, the VERD-IE drones were created by the Orokin to tend to the numerous agri-biomes and vegetation all around the Zariman. After the ship was lost within the Void and the agri-biomes vented into space, the drones worked tirelessly to reseal and replant them, and now are awaiting the next harvest.

## Trivia

- A specific VERD-IE unit, named PRIMM, can be found accompanying Hombask, and will occasionally make chattering and beeping sounds.
- "VERD-IE" is derived from the word "verdant" meaning "lush with green vegetation."
