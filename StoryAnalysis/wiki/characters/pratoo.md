## Lore

Pratoo wanted Little Duck to sell arms to the Tenno in Fortuna, but she refused the offer in favor of a contract to extract Jubb Lott, a Corpus defector. She underestimated Pratoo's ability to divine the future, however. The information about the Orb Mothers that the contract revealed ultimately had her abandon treasure hunting and return to Fortuna to help Solaris United.

Now Little Duck is selling Vox Solaris wares to the Tenno, which include Amp parts, just like Pratoo wanted.
