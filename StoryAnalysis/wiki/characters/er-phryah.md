> Er-Phryah is a prominent figure from Ostron folklore as told in Tale of the Woman of the Earth and the Husband of the Sea. She is a young Ostron woman from the yingbindunyai clade, "a very old and wealthy compact of bonded families", who is in love with a young cladeless Ostron man named Mer-Sah.
