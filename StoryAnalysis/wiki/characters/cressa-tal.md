## Lore

### The Pyrus Project

Wanting to repay the Tenno for their aid, Cressa Tal begins a campaign project to rebuild the Relays destroyed by Councilor Vay Hek's Balor Fomorians during Operation: Eyes of Blight. This, however, earns the ire of General Sargas Ruk, who reveals that Cressa Tal previously served him and harbors a strong grudge for her betrayal, and leads a siege on the rebuilt Relay.
