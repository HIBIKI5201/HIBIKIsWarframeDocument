## Lore

### The Old Peace

Daklo does not hide his dislike towards the Sentients; he refers to Caliban Prime as "Orokin gold - wasted!" and an "aberration", and openly insults Adis by calling him "void-lugger". He later approaches the Operator as they try to find Adis, who was saddened by them killing a controlled Dactolyst, expressing his confusions that the Sentients wish to be human.

Despite this, Daklo is shocked to witness the Grineer forces immediately turning on the Sentients the moment Ballas breaks the treaty. The Operator, on a blind rage, can choose to spare or attack Daklo.
