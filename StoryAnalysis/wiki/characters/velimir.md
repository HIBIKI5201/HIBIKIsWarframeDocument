## Lore & History

Velimir is a former Libertatian agent. His father was originally from Rus, but he moved to Libertatia after defecting from his country. He met Minerva at a spy conference, where he was instantly smitten by her and attempted to woo her with cold pickup lines and alcohol; though she was not impressed by the former, the latter allowed their relationship to begin forming. They continued to bond after spending six weeks together after crash landing on a deserted island.

The pair met Neci Rusalka as a war orphan while on an assignment and adopted her.

Despite Minerva requesting that Rusalka not join the Scaldra, as she had evidence on their misdeeds, Velimir gave Rusalka his blessing as Minerva was unable to share the full scope of her evidence, given that it would have greatly endangered them, causing their relationship to fall apart as a result.

While on the search for Rusalka, they arrived in Höllvania a year prior while disguised as corporate sales reps and willingly took Albrecht Entrati's Protoframe injections. Unlike the original members of the Hex, however, Entrati was honest about what the Protoframe injections would do to them. Neither were aware that Rusalka had lost her arm.

### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Minerva, Velimir

Velimir's username is "PapaPolar" and is paired in a group chat with his wife Minerva "MomToxicated". In contrast to his ruthless wife, Velimir is surprisingly level-headed with the Drifter and likes to joke around as well as tell stories. That said, he will immediately get protective of Rusalka if the Drifter hints having a sexual attraction to her.

He is also very fond of alcohol and discovers he can outdrink Arthur. He also shares a liking for singing and cooking with the latter. He later attempts to make BBQ out of Techrot which backfires horribly.

It is shown that he likes to smoke and that Rusalka got that habit from him. He also seems to be slightly claustrophobic.

During conversations, it is stated he was the emotional support of the family and allowed Rusalka to join the Scaldra to distance himself from his father's raising methods and to let her make her own choices. He clarifies his father never really approved anything he did, other than marrying Minerva, and he refused to do the same to his daughter.

Due to his relationship with Minerva, Velimir cannot be romanced by the Drifter.

The Drifter can attempt to fix their marriage. Certain routes will reveal that either feel like a burden, which will eventually allow them to reconcile. Other routes might have either of them to consider the other a burden and as a result, the Drifter can reveal that Velimir attempts to cope through the situation with humor but must be careful not to antagonize either of them, leading to them having one date to work on their issues for old times sake. Otherwise, they are given one last chance to amend the situation by pointing out that they still entertain the KIM conversations and then ask for a story of them working together. If the marriage counseling is successful, the pair will now be facing each other in the Höllvania Central Mall and make flirtatious remarks during subsequent chats and on the bar afterwards, with Velimir becoming even friendlier when interacted with. On the other hand, if the Drifter fails to repair their relationship or simply decides not to intervene, the pair's conversations become increasingly distant and much shorter, with Velimir not bothering to send any messages during their rank 6 chat.

If their marriage is fixed, on their final chat, Velimir tells the Drifter to tell Rusalka, should they ever encounter her again: "On empty crossroads, seek the eclipse."

If the player finds all Isleweaver fragments, they will receive a message: "For when Sol and Lua align, the lost shall find their way home." If they also fixed the marriage between Minerva and Velimir, the Drifter reveals Rusalka's current situation with the couple explainaining these are code phrases used between them to comunicate safely. The couple interpret that the message is indeed from Rusalka herself and they should not worry about her for the time being. Velimir will also thank the Drifter for delivering the mesage and will consider the latter part of their family from that moment.

Several conversations with The Hex (After Isleweaver) show he is very friendly in a general sense, with all of them enjoying his presence in their lives and him showing a great deal of concern for them as well, the Drifter included. In particular, he is shown to have become somewhat closer to Arthur, as both of them have similar interests. According to Eleanor, he seems to be filling the role of a father figure for the latter, to the point of stating Velimir would be the father Arthur would have picked if he was in any position to do so. She even compares Velimir's and Minerva's bickering to the twins's own parents, which might make Arthur feel at home somewhat.

## Trivia

- Designed by Liger Inuzuka, who also designed the Nidus Phryke, Nezha Empyrean, Excalibur Zato, Styanax Tonatiuh and Kullervo Apostate Skins.
- He has the latest birthday of the Hex, on December 21st.
- On one of his chats, he compares his first meeting with Minerva to an old cartoon where a male skunk falls in love with a female cat that he thinks is a female skunk. This is a reference to the character "Pepe Le Pew" from The Looney Tunes franchise.
- According to Quincy, like himself and Minerva, he is also fan of audiobooks. However, he seems more interested in the war genre rather than romance, as he mentions his favorite being a novel called "Alien Warlords from Mars".
