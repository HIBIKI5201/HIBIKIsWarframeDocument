## Appearance

Kalymos is quite unique-looking compared to other Kavat breeds. She is completely hairless except for her whiskers, leaving her pale green skin bare. Her eyes uniquely glow solid blue, without distinction of sclera and pupil, similar to the rest of House Entrati's members. She also has a single tail with a fan-shaped tip.

Kalymos wears golden ornaments on her shoulders, and bracelets on her limbs and tail. Her ears are adorned with golden curved earpieces. She wears a long collar adorned with Entrati Obols.

## Lore & History

### General

Unlike with other animals, Albrecht didn’t send Kalymos into Void expeditions due to sentimental values and her intelligence, although he does mention it would’ve been more ethical to use her as opposed to the last pair of Cervulites.

According to Loid, Kalymos was once very sick and was kept in seclusion for some time, but later mysteriously returned healthy. Loid speculates that Kalymos may have been cloned.

### Whispers in the Walls

"The Kalymos Sequence" is named after the Kavat.

Kalymos traveled to the year of 1999 alongside her master.

### The Lotus Eaters

When the Lotus instructs the Drifter to answer the call on the ringing pager in Sanctum Anatomica, they are suddenly warped to an abandoned mall. The Drifter then encounters Kalymos, who leads them around the abandoned mall, eventually leading them to another ringing pager before disappearing.

### The Hex

Kalymos is first encountered by Arthur alongside Albrecht Entrati who then vanishes. She later appears in the Höllvania Central Mall to guide the Drifter to the Backroom where they receive a message from her master.

After the quest, Kalymos continues to hang around the Central Mall, though some of the Hex (especially Leticia, fearing Kalymos may go after her rats) remain wary of her. She also provides assistance in hunting the Legacyte for Legacyte Harvest.

## Trivia

- The name Kalymos is likely derived from the name Kalydnos, one of the titans in Greek mythology. Furthermore, Kalymos' traits of resilience and fortitude derive the mythos associated with this titan, one who clung onto an island to save himself from falling into Tartarus. Alternatively, Kalymos may have been named after the Greek island of Kalymnos.
- The general concept of Kalymos appears to derive from Bubastis, the faithful evolved cat of Watchmen's morally compromised anti-villain, Ozymandias.

A Kalymos-themed Kavat Gene Masking Kit can be purchased from the Market for 55 and included in Sanctum Supporter Pack.

  - While Kalymos is a female kavat, the kavat in Sanctum Supporter Pack is a male Vasca Kavat.
- In Amir's New Years Kiss cutscene, he throws his glasses away, causing a cat's yowling.
- If the player is not in a relationship with any of the Hex members but is Best Friends with all six of them by New Years, they will get a unique inbox message from Kalymos (though it is actually signed by Arthur) and a cutscene featuring the Drifter sharing a hug with Kalymos during New Years.
