## Lore & History

### Background

> “If you were to consider yourself a coward for this choice, I beseech you - do not. For this is a pain that is not yours to face. It is mine. You did not ask to bear this moment.

> This mechanical contraption at which we sit creates an impression of veiled impersonality. That we are somehow sheltered from whatever harm another might cause us through whatever words they type to us upon it. I would argue instead the opposite - that free of the armor we don in our more mundane social interactions, we are stripped of that which might protect us. Therefore, in situations like these... I am well aware that I let loose an arrow that might find not the battle-ready soldier, but the unsuspecting jugular of a deer in the woods. So I shall, therefore, leave you with this: We must remind ourselves how to find the strength to pull another to their feet, even when we cannot find the will to stand. That is the true purpose of story. Be well, Drifter. We shall speak again soon. -F. Lyon Allard” —Lyon, after selecting the option to avoid topics of self-harm

Lyon's grandparents (whether maternal or paternal is unknown) met in the Gaullique Revolution. He and his parents lived and worked on a farm, with a small village nearby.

Content Warning: Self-Harm, Child Abuse ▾▾

From an early age, Lyon has experienced visions, visible only to him and often terrifying in nature. The earliest example he brings up, when he was around four to six years old, was of an old, withered woman with wispy hair and missing eyes, whom he awoke to find sitting at the foot of his bed. He informed his mother of the vision that night and when, three months later, a woman matching this description was found dead in her home, Lyon's mother took what he had seen to be the work of the Devil. She would beat him with a switch while calling him "Devil" and "Sorcerer" until he could neither stand nor sleep on his back, something it is implied she did from then on with some regularity. He also mentions to The Drifter that his father was even worse, altough he doesn't elaborate on what his father did to him.

As he grew older, these visions developed into voices that he heard at all hours, whether awake or asleep. His work on the family farm began to suffer, which in turn worsened his parents' treatment of him. The visions and voices often sent him into violent fits, from which he could only shake himself through acts of self-harm.

One night, when Lyon was approximately seventeen years of age, he experienced a vision in which shadowy figures demanded he kill his parents. When he regained awareness he found himself stood at the door of his parents' room, knife in hand, and was so overwhelmed he fled into the woods - even though by his own admition he felt no love for his parents at that point, he was still unable to kill them. Terrified of what the visions had almost driven him to do, Lyon attempted to fix the problem by gouging out his own eyes, reasoning that if he could not see the visions, they would no longer affect him. His attempt failed as he lost consciousness after stabbing the knife into his left eye socket.

He awoke in the nearby parish, his injuries tended to by a visiting Brother of Lua. Overcome, Lyon told the Brother of his visions and what they had demanded of him, expecting to be condemned for his sins. Instead the Brother gave him an amulet of Lua and sent him to the Cathedral in the Cité de la Lumière, where he would stay and join the priesthood. During his stay there, Lyon was introduced to the practice of self-flagellation by one of the other Brothers, and began using it to clear his mind when the visions grew too much to bear.

At some point during his time at the Cathedral, he met Marie and the pair grew close, considering each other to be brother and sister.

When Albrecht Entrati arrived upon the Cathedral's doorstep under the identity of "Cardinal Dimanche", Lyon thought little of it until the Cardinal suddenly vanished, a little over three years after his arrival, returning a year later with an already-transformed Roathe. Entrati explained who he was, from where and when he had come, and what task he wished Marie and Lyon to carry out, along with the Protoframe serum for both Harrow and Wisp.

Both Marie and Lyon accepted the serum and the task Entrati set for them, watching over Roathe and ascertaining whether he - and by extension the Orokin as a whole - could ever be redeemed. However, their transformations had rendered the trio unable to go out in public without heavy disguises, so they were instead kept hidden within the crypts out of sight, only communicating with Marie's spectral manifestations. This continued for an unknown length of time until Roathe reverse-engineered a Helminth that brought the three of them to the Sanctum Anatomica, where they now reside within a replica of the Cathedral's crypts.

### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messenger/Lyon

Lyon initially refuses to speak to the Drifter over KIM, only beginning to message them once they have proven themselves to Marie by befriending her and reaching 'Liked' rank. He is initially taciturn and distant, gradually warming up to the Drifter and trusting them with more information about Roathe, Marie, their history together and his own past.

Lyon signs off almost all of his conversations as "F. Lyon Allard" at first, graduating to simply "Lyon" as he grows closer to the Drifter.

As Lyon's backstory involves self-harm and child abuse, several of his conversations come with a content warning which, if selected, will end the conversation early and skip future discussions on the topic or modify certain lines to remove direct references to it. No Chemistry is lost by doing this.

Lyon's arc within KIM is about resolving a faith crisis, which is related to his past and specifically regarding Rell, the original Operator of the Warframe Harrow, who somehow was present in Lyon's life since he was a child, along with The Man in the Wall. He explains his perception of times is not linear the way it's preceived by any other person - he will mention remembering several key moments of his past with Rell guiding him and conforting him, while he also remembers another version where he was alone.

## Trivia

He has Polycoria in his exposed right eye, which is a real life condition where the affected eye has more than one pupillary opening in the iris. This is a reference to his template Harrow, which has two eye holes on the right side of his base skin's default helmet. Likewise, the eyepatch covering his left eye-socket has 3 golden discs, echoing Harrow's helmet having the same trait.

  - In the real world, polycoria manifests as the presence of multiple sphincter muscles in the iris ("true polycoria") or tearing of the iris that results in additional holes ("pseudopolycoria"). The presence of multiple complete irises (conjoined or not) as Lyon has is a popular depiction of the medical condition in fiction and can be seen in many digitally altered images, but such an occurrence has never been verifiably reported.
- When encountered as a defense target during The Descendia he will wield his Warframe's Signature Weapons, Scourge and Knell.
- While The Drifter's notes on Lyon mention he is easier to anger than Marie, this is actually not the case. In fact, while being a lot more reserved and serious than she is, he is also a lot more patient and as a result, it's more difficult to trip up with him in chat than it is with Marie.

Lyon mentions to the Drifter that, after seeing what the protoframe serum did to Roathe (Melting the latters armor around his flesh), he decided to lock himself in his room, strip completely and move away from any objects before injecting himself.

  - He also remarks he is the protoframe that could blend into a crowd the best, as he is the least visibly infested of all protoframes (With the exception of maybe Velimir), with his back and forearms being very easy to conceal with enough clothing.

When purchasing Tektolyst Artifacts from Marie a cutscene will play where Lyon is seen pulling the Artifact from the Void.

  - Upon purchasing the Lorak Tektolyst Artifact Lyon will invoke the name of Rell, the original user of the Harrow Warframe.
  - An inactive Donda can be seen on Lyon's table, further implying the relations between Lyon and Rell.
  - In the Lyon's Cold Below Date Scene, Lyon says that he met Rell and Harrow there before.
- Lyon suffers both from fear of heights and motion sickness, and will react with distress whenever a vehicle is summoned while using his Gemini Skin. He also has arachnophobia (fear of spiders) and will comment on this when fighting Oraxia in The Descendia.
- When facing the Technocyte Coda in The Descendia, Lyon will mention he dislikes On-lyne's music, referring to it as "noise" and claiming nothing has shaken his faith more than listening to it. He also doesn't seem to like 1999's music as a whole.
