## Lore & History

### Zariman ARG

Kira featured prominently in the Zariman ARG event, as she is one of the Holdfasts who took the risk of attempting to recover logs made by her husband describing the events surrounding the process of her going insane from Void exposure and subsequent death. She regrettably suffered the consequences and succumbed to the song, which transformed her into the Void Angel.

### Angels of the Zariman

After fully succumbing to the influence of the Void, Kira reappears as a fully fledged Void Angel. First seen roosting in the ceiling of the Zariman, Kira stuns the Tenno, leading to the rest of the Holdfasts to rescue them. After repairing the Zariman's Exolizers, Kira attempts to make her way to the the Reliquary Drive and feed off of it. However, she is ultimately slain by the Tenno.

## Trivia

Kira has her own Shawzin that is purchaseable as a furnishing with Platinum at the Market.

  - The Void's Song Shawzin appears to be a Void-corrupted version of her shawzin.
- Prior to Update 31.6 (2022-06-09), Kira's Memorial featured "A Lost Time" portrait decoration meant to honor all who were lost in the Zariman Void Jump. However, this led to confusion that Kira was biologically related to the player's Operator. The portrait has since been replaced with Kira's Shawzin.
