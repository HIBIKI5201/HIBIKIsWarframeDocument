## Lore

The Myrmidon wielded a wrist-worn device of Orokin origin that enabled him to disintegrate Warframes with a mere touch, most likely by harnessing the destructive power of the emerald Jade Light. This same device also enabled the Myrmidon to disable Warframe powers from a distance with only a gesture of his hand.

After defeating and subduing him, Ivara dragged the Myrmidon before the assembled Orokin Council of Executors to face judgement, only for him to be revealed as merely a vessel controlled remotely via Transference by one of the Executors. Ballas then revealed that the entire hunt was merely a test of Ivara's battle prowess and loyalty - one which she had passed while her compatriots had failed.
