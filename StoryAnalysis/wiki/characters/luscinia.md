## Lore

Luscinia is Dominus Thrax's favorite singer; the envious Bombastine once tried to break her but instead made her Dominus Thrax's chosen; she now serves as his primary entertainer. A deeply melancholic and depressed woman who indulges in excessive sorrow, she frequently harasses Thrax and the rest of Duviri out of spite; however, the guilt from her actions drives her further into depression.

Luscinia is fully aware of why Bombastine harasses her and genuinely shares his sentiment, as she wishes to trade places with him as well.

### Lost Islands of Duviri

There was a time when Duviri's festivals were held on Lake Verula as its main spot. Within the lake, a school of rainbow eels would be attracted by the music and would follow Luscinia across, floundering on the shore when she disembarked, as if they yearned to remain with her.

### Tales of Duviri - The Sorrowful Soprano

> “Sorrow is the bitter spice without which life is too sweet. Luscinia indulged in it…to excess.”

> —Euleria Entrati

Luscinia indulged in sorrow in excess. It is a poison that Luscinia drank like wine. She wondered if inflicting suffering upon those who deserved it would end her misery, but she eventually realized that though it comforted her at first, it was a cold comfort and her sorrow grew. Sorrow made Luscinia compassionate for she could not give pain without feeling it and she wept for those she was hurting. She tried to dismiss this thought as a weakness but her heart disagreed. Luscinia eventually decided to walk down the dark path she had set her feet upon to the very end. She already knew the outcome, like a prophet's gift, but she could not turn aside, even if she prepared to risk everything on a reckless gamble.

## Trivia

- Luscinia refers to a genus of birds that contain nightingales. Bombastine occasionally rants that she is "still chirping away on her perch of honor" as well as having an unused dialog calling her a "song bird", possibly an intentional or coincidental reference to her namesake.
- Her name appears in the title of a Decree, Luscinia's Suffering.
- During her Spiral, Luscinia will have three drops of water on her forehead in her transmission.
- Luscinia appears to be self-aware that she is a fictional character, as she knows that she was written as a sad character (as seen in the quote above). This trait is shared with Dominus Thrax, who also expressed self-awareness in similar ways.
