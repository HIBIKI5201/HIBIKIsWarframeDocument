## Trivia

- Aoi uses Zeke's photo as her KIM avatar.
- Zeke is a Big Bytes Pizza advertisement presenter with Pepperoni with Extra Cheese as his favorite Byte. His Coda counterpart, on the other hand, always talk about how he is strongly fed up with Big Byte's pizza and its cheese to the point just thought makes him want to hurl and he plans to try being a vegan after the contract ends. This can be a reference to some sponsor companies that place restriction on their presenters to use only the products they advertise with for entire contract duration and might supply them with abnormally large amount of the product.
