## Lore & History

Delivering Fergolyte to The Prince will cause it to grow, which unlocks a vision that reveals Nightcap's history with Nokko. In this vision, players control a Sprodling guided by the voices and images of characters and growing mushrooms stalks.

20 Fergolyte unlocks the Purple Stem Path, 40 for the Blue Stem Path, and 60 for the Green Stem Path, for a total of 120 Fergolyte.

### Purple Stem Path

Nightcap is introduced willingly working for Nef Anyo, clearing out the native fungi that grow beneath the Vallis surface to make room for Corpus expansion. Nightcap and his fellow Solaris are led to believe that the fungi are an Orokin experiment gone awry. After some time, they discover a "kid" deep in the mines who regularly sabotages their equipment.

### Blue Stem Path

Despite his antics, Nightcap grows attached to the "kid" and names him Nokko (short for "No Corporate", or "No Co"). Nightcap learns that the Vallis fungi feed off waste products generated by Venus's cooling towers: without them, Venus would quickly revert to its normal, scorching climate. Despite Nef Anyo's demands, Nightcap shuts down the elevator leading to the Deepmines.

### Green Stem Path

Nightcap discovers that Nokko is part of the Vallis fungi, defending his home and by extension, the Vallis above. Disappointed by Nightcap's resistance, Nef Anyo sends Corpus troops to breach the elevator. Nightcap responds by blowing up the mineshaft, denying any access going to and from the Deepmines. Cut off from resources, Nightcap's condition worsens while Nokko chooses to remain by his side. Nightcap is last seen laying on a bed of fungi, bidding Nokko farewell.

Dialogue from Nightcap stating he is physically unable to leave and Nef Anyo being unable to find Nightcap's name in debt ledgers implies that Nightcap starved to death and was revived by Nokko to continue defending the Deepmines. However, Nightcap himself denies this, warning the Tenno that The Prince can play tricks with their mind.
