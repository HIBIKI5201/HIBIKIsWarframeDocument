## Lore & History

### Ash's Leverian

Pilio and his brother, Dom, were both members of the Orokin school of assassination known as the Scoria. While Dom was gifted in assassination, Pilio was not so, but he nonetheless revered Ash, the Scoria's patron Warframe.

During one mission to Mars, Dom was captured by the target he was meant to kill. In Scoria Doctrine, this was an unforgivable mistake, so Pilio was sent to kill both the original target and Dom, to confirm to the Scoria that his brother's fault was not part of the family. Ash was sent with him to oversee the mission, and kill Pilio if he failed. During this mission, Pilio showed restraint and fear, then disgust at the corpses of two guards he had killed. As Pilio and Ash approached the courtyard, they were attacked by reinforcements. Ash put the boy behind cover and efficiently killed the fifty guards facing them while Pilio averted his gaze from the bodies. Ash led Pilio to the chamber where the original target was staying, and was shocked to find Dom with him, in civilian clothes, sharing a drink and missing the black gem of the Scoria between his eyes. Pilio saw images in his mind, which his gem was suppressing, and Dom asked his brother to listen to the target. The target grabbed his gun, only to be restrained by Ash. But when Pilio asked them "why?", Ash released his grip and removed Pilio's gem from his face with a talon.

The Scoria gem had been suppressing Pilio's memories, and now he could recall that the target was actually his own father, Lio De Nas. Ash let Pilio decide between the Scoria and his family. Pilio dropped his knife, and his father took him and his brother away, fleeing Mars entirely, while Ash simply waited in silence. During The Collapse, all of the Scoria were killed by Ash himself. Pilio would later tell his account of the story, which was archived in the Leverian.
