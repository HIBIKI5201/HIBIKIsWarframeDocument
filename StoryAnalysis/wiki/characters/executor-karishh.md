## Lore & History

### General

The glutton Karishh was a very large and corpulent Orokin that possessed twelve additional, grafted-on stomachs, requiring the use of exo-sac levitators to support them and move around.

### Karishh's Final Feast

Main article: Leverian#Grendel

After the Collapse of the Orokin Empire, Karishh found refuge within the frozen, famine-struck city of Riddha, located on Europa. There he would live a lavish life, consuming feast after feast while the emaciated citizens looked on.

The Warframes Grendel and Gauss would then appear to liberate Riddha from Karishh's control. Gauss would remain near the entrance of the city, presumably intercepting any returning patrols, while Grendel would go on to plow straight into the city, consuming all of Karishh's sycophants and Dax soldiers. With his gut full, Grendel would then Nourish all the afflicted masses of Riddha with the power he consumed, leading them to join him in storming the Karishh's manse.

Smashing open the gates with his Masseter, Grendel would lead the newly reinvigorated citizens, interrupting yet another of Karishh's feasts. The Executor would then flee to the far-off mountains of Riddha, pursued by Grendel. As the citizens sat feasting, Grendel would have a feast of his own, consuming Karishh in one bite.

## Trivia

- The Mines of Karishh is an Excavation Mission where players can earn Grendel systems.
- Karishh's name is spelled two different ways in-game. The Exo-Sac Archwing Skin's description calls him "Karissh", while Grendel's Leverian and his dinnerware decoration calls him "Karishh".
