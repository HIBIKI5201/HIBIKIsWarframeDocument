### Kinemantik Instant Messenger

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messenger/Roathe

According to Roathe, he was part of the first expeditionary crew to the Tau System after the completion of the Solar Rail connecting it to the Origin System.

His leadership over the expedition to Tau is said to be a consequence of the Zariman Ten Zero incident, whom which Executor Tuvul was a patron to.

There are no records about his fate after the fall of the Orokin Empire, but he is presumed dead.
