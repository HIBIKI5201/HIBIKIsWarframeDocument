## Lore

### The Old Peace

Itzam was the teacher of the Operator and Adis in the Tauron Academy. However, he was also concerned about the Anarchs and the fragile stability of the peace between the Orokin and Sentient.

When Ballas personally arrived on Tauron Academy, Itzam advised the Operator not to confront him and went to speak with the Executor alone. Itzam requested Ballas deal with the Anarchs, revealing to him a weakness in the World-Seeder bases that the Anarchs are occupying.

Itzam oversaw the Operator's and Adis's Tauron Graduation and bestowed them an Ayatan from Margulis, but the ceremony was interrupted by Anarch-aligned Grineer. Itzam boarded a Parallax, but the landing craft was suddenly shot down before it could escape.

## Trivia

He is distinguished from the Summulyst enemies by his blue accents and less aggressive eyes. As a non-combatant he also lacks the guns on his hands and wears glasses instead.

  - Due to his role, it is unknown if he can summon Choralysts like the other Summulysts can.

Itzam is currently the only seen Archimedian to be explicitly non-human and non-Orokin.

  - Suda (and presumably Otak) were humans during their times as an Archimedian before being converted into a Cephalon.
  - Ballas refers to him as the first Sentient Archimedian, inferring there being no other Sentient Archimedians during Itzam's time.
- Itzam's Oculus can be purchased as an Operator Accessory for 75 Platinum.
