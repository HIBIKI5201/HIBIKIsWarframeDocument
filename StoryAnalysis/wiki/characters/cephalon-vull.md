## 概要

Conclave の Cephalon Capture に登場

> Cephalon Vull is one of the Cephalons in Conclave Cephalon Capture mode. Vull is characterized by a sadistic personality expressed in an otherwise cheery tone. She does not seem particularly choosy as to which team she prefers, and will congratulate Tenno playing for either the Sun or Moon team as long as they kill others in her presence.
> Cephalon Vull is voiced by Carin Gilfry.
