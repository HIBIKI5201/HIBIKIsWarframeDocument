## Lore & History

### The Seven Crimes of Kullervo

Main article: Kullervo#Lore

> “You cannot deny your sin, Kullervo. For I am your guilt, the form and voice of your greatest persecutor.”

> —The Warden

The Warden is presumed to be a Conceptual Embodiment of Kullervo's guilt, taking the form and voice of Ballas, one of the Orokin Executors whom charged Kullervo with his seven crimes. Kullervo would then be punished with the Executor's eternal vengeance.

According to Acrithis, the Warden claims to originate outside the Void, and to be more than a Conceptual Embodiment. Acrithis herself does not believe him.
