## Lore

### Tau

With the Tenno soon arriving to the Tau System, Albrecht Entrati has Brysko infiltrate the Hunra's organization as a double agent, knowing that keeping the Hunra in power will inevitably lead to war. The Hunra meanwhile monologues to himself about how the rain at Fornax used to "wipe the mistakes out of the day" but now "come clutching [the past] back up", and later shrugs off rumors of him trading his heart with "the Devil" for his current power.

As Brysko is about to meet with the Hunra, a scene shows Adis being manipulated by The Indifference into believing the Operator betrayed him and stole his light. As the scene zooms in on Adis' face, he opens his eyes before the scene suddenly cuts to The Hunra's face and zooming out.

The Hunra offers to play Portau, wagering with secrets of what is coming back to Tau, the Blooms, and Brysko's maker. However, the Hunra threatens to take his core should he lose.

## Trivia

- The Hunra's smaller, true body bears a striking resemblance to Adis' own, albeit much battered and has different arms. While the demo hinted some connections between them, it is currently unknown whether the two are the same or if the Hunra is controlling Adis' corpse much like Pazuul does to Erra's corpse.
