## 概要

Orokin が設計した巨大な生体機械

> The Orowyrms are gigantic bio-mechanical creatures of Orokin design. They serve as the Grand Bosses of the Duviri Landscape, fought at the final stage of a Spiral.
> Each of the courtiers of Dominus Thrax has an Orowyrm form they assume when they lose control over the emotion they embody: Mathila for Joy, Lodun for Anger, Bombastine for Envy, Luscinia for Sorrow, and Sythel for Fear. The only gameplay difference between these Orowyrms is the elemental damage they inflict.
> They were officially introduced in Update 29.0 (2020-08-25), appearing inside mirrors found in Isolation Vaults. Orowyrms have since made appearances in other places as well, making rare occurrences while loading in Empyrean Void Storms and regularly passing by in the Void passage between Rania and Belric's crystals in Mirror Defense.

## Trivia

- They were first seen in The Duviri Paradox Reveal Trailer on TennoCon 2019.

The Orowyrm is sometimes referred to by the community as "Void Worm" or "Space Dragon", due to its serpentine appearance.

  - Fittingly enough, a "wyrm" is a type of dragon, usually depicted without any limbs.
- Early in its development as seen in its concept art, the Orowyrm was known as the Tubificid.
- An Orowyrm can be seen at the side of the Railjack at the beginning of an Empyrean void fissure missions
- Mathila and Lodun imply that Orowyrm transformation is not voluntary, instead happening when the courtiers lose control of their emotions.
- When closely examining the image of Pathos Clamp, it could be assumed that, like any other large Orokin construct, there is organic matter under the shell of the Orowyrm.
- They may have appeared even earlier in the game than their official form - a similar serpentine silhouette could rarely be seen moving past the blue glowing "window" panels within Orokin Towers. This may have been the design prototype of their official appearance later within Isolation Vault mirrors.
- Aiming at an Orowyrm's head in its second phase will cause a smaller health bar with Lodun's name to appear, regardless of the courtier and their associated spiral.
- The Orowrym used during The Duviri Paradox quest deals Tau Damage against the other Orowryms.

Lead Writer Adrian Bott revealed during Prime Time and in one of his X (formerly Twitter) posts that all other named residents in Duviri (including the prisoner appearing in stages but not The Warden) embodies aspects of different emotions much like the courtiers, although it is unknown if they have their own Orowyrm form, given their low-ranking status and therefore lacking their respective spirals. These characters included:

  - Acrithis - Curiosity
  - Brimon - Pride
  - Koral - Compassion
  - Barris - Frustration
  - The Bard (Pip) - Loneliness
  - The Prisoner - Optimism
- During the beginning of The Duviri Paradox quest, Dominus Thrax summoned an Orowyrm with golden accents and blue lights not matching with any of the five courtiers in Duviri. A second Orowyrm with red patterns and lights identical to Lodun also appeared near the end of said quest.
- An Orowyrm is utilised by Ryoku during The Kuva Wytch mission in Uranus Proxima, which he mentioned it being a secret weapon of the Orokin.
