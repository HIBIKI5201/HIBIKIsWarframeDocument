## Lore & History

Minerva is a former Libertatian military prodigy. She met Velimir at a spy conference, who was instantly smitten by her and attempted to woo her with cold-themed pickup lines and alcohol; while she was only barely amused by the former, the latter worked to win her trust. They continued to bond after spending six weeks together after crash landing on a deserted island.

The pair met Neci Rusalka as a war orphan while on an assignment and adopted her.

Minerva also carried out investigations into the Scaldra mercenary group, stockpiling evidence of their misconduct, and explicitly requested for Rusalka to avoid joining them with the evidence in mind. However, as she could not share the exact details with either Velimir or Rusalka as it would endanger them, Velimir gave Rusalka his blessing to join the Scaldra. This would consequently put strain on their relationship.

While on their search for Rusalka, they arrived in Höllvania a year prior while disguised as corporate sales reps and willingly took Albrecht Entrati's Protoframe injections. Unlike the original members of the Hex, however, Entrati was honest about what the Protoframe injections would do to them. Neither were aware that Rusalka had lost her arm.

### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Minerva, Velimir

Minerva's username is "MomToxicated" and is paired in a group chat with her husband Velimir "PapaPolar". Owing to her background, Minerva is quite ruthless and expects immediate results, an attitude that the Drifter quickly does not appreciate of, especially when Minerva implies The Drifter was responsible of Rusalka's fate. She later changes her approach, though remains reluctant to share information that she considers "classified." As a battle-hardened soldier, she prefers responses that are blunt and straight to the point. That said, it is shown she is not above of using her looks to her advantage, something she instilled in Rusalka while raising her.

Due to her relationship with Velimir, Minerva cannot be romanced by the Drifter.

The Drifter can attempt to fix their marriage. Certain routes will reveal that either feel like a burden, which will eventually allow them to reconcile. Other routes might have either of them to consider the other a burden and as a result, the Drifter can reveal that Velimir attempts to cope through the situation with humor but must be careful not to antagonize either of them, leading to them having one date to work on their issues for old times sake. Otherwise, they are given one last chance to amend the situation by pointing out that they still entertain the KIM conversations and then ask for a story of them working together. If the marriage counseling is successful, the pair will now be facing each other in the Höllvania Central Mall and make flirtatious remarks both during subsequent chats and on the bar afterwards, with Minerva becoming friendlier when interacted with and being more willing to share personal info with the Drifter. On the other hand, if the Drifter fails to repair their relationship or simply decides not to intervene, the pair's conversations become increasingly distant and much shorter, with Minerva reflecting that not all stories have a happy ending in their rank 6 chat.

Regardless of the outcome, she admits in that chat that she has come to like The Mall, remarking she enjoys the company of the Hex and The Drifter, even mentioning she is starting a reading club along Eleanor, Leticia and Aoi.

If the player finds all Isleweaver fragments, they will receive a message: "For when Sol and Lua align, the lost shall find their way home." If they also fixed the marriage between Minerva and Velimir, the Drifter reveals Rusalka's current situation with the couple explaining these are code phrases used between them to comunicate safely. The couple interpret that the message is indeed from Rusalka herself and they should not worry about her for the time being. Minerva will also thank the Drifter for delivering the mesage and everything they've done up to that point.

## Trivia

- Minerva can speak multiple languages due to being stationed overseas so many times. During one conversation she will cuss out Velimir in Romanian.
- Minerva's name may derive from the Roman goddess of wisdom and strategic warfare, both references to her military background.
- According to Quincy, like himself, she likes romance audiobooks (And apparently she has a rather large collection of CDs), with Minerva suggesting trading some of them with the former, much to his embarrassment.
- Prior to her introduction, the community initially believed the Protoframe Saryn to be Neci Rusalka due to her design and Efervon aesthetics until Devstream 183 debunked it.
- During Temporal Archimidea, when Velimir suggests they get a cat for the bar after taking a liking to Kalymos, Minerva reminds him she is allergic.
