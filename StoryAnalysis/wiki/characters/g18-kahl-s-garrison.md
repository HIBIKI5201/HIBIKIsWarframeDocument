#### Trivia

Unlike other Grineer Ballista, Jarka does not wear a helmet or mask, and she wields an Ignis instead of a Vulkar.

  - Her default Ignis can be switched for weapon of player's choice upon hiring Jarka as crewmember.
- Jarka's voice line "Okay. Does everyone see the Tenno? Listen. Jarka has dealt with them before. Wait until it starts mining. Or fishing. Then attack!" references a long-running gag in the community where enemies in Landscape missions would spawn and constantly attack players during the aforementioned non-combat activities. This running gag was first present in Update 22.0 (2017-10-12) which introduced the first landscape, the Plains of Eidolon.
- Jarka is voiced by Australian actor Gemma Laurelle.
