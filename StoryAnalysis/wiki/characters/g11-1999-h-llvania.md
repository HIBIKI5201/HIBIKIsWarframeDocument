### 詳細

#### 人物

ブリタニック陸軍の退役中尉で、The Hex のリーダー。The Hex は Höllvania を拠点とする反体制の自警団で、1999 年、Techrot の感染爆発や敵対する Scaldra の軍と戦っている。

##### Protoframe 調書

- 通称: 「BROADSWORD」
- 役割: リーダー、戦闘指揮
- 特徴・癖: 高度に規律正しく、職業軍人としての訓練を受けている。奇妙なことに、仲間以外との個人的な付き合いが知られていない。
- 評判: 気まぐれ？ 実証済みの指導力、示された献身、有能な現場指揮。それでも暴れ馬と見なされている……
- 特記事項: 衝突しながらも姉に並外れて忠実。グループのまとまりにとって問題になるかもしれない。

#### 経歴

##### 生い立ち

ブリタニック出身の Arthur は、Eleanor とともに牧歌的な子ども時代を過ごし、一度も喧嘩をしなかった。Eleanor はよく Arthur の髪を編んだり化粧をしたりした。二人には毎年ともに悼む記念日があり、その日に大切な人を失っている。

Arthur はブリタニック陸軍の Crimson Watch で、Eleanor の婚約者 Christopher の下で働いていた。Christopher が道路脇の爆弾で死んだため Arthur が昇進し、その直後に Quincy が Arthur の下に配属された。ある任務で「敵の戦闘員になりうる者全員」を殺せ（おそらく罪のない民間人も含まれた）と命じられた Arthur は上官に抗議し、不名誉除隊をちらつかされた。これに対し彼は、名誉除隊にするか、Eleanor のつてを使って彼らの行いを暴かれるかを選べと最後通告を突きつけた。結果、彼は名誉除隊となり、Quincy も後に続いた。

Höllvania に来る前、Arthur は Aoi とともに国際危機対応（ICR）部隊に所属し、任務の一環でこの都市国家に送られた。Arthur が Höllvania での出来事を Eleanor に伝えたことで、彼女は調査・報道のために違法にこの地域へ入った。Arthur は ICR なら民間人にもっと同情的だろうと期待していたが、感染の宿主になりうる者は誰であれ殺せと Aoi とともに命じられ、その期待は裏切られた。

やがて彼自身も Techrot に感染し、Dr. Entrati に治療を求めた。

##### Protoframe の実験

Arthur と Aoi は、治療以上のものを受けるという Dr. Entrati の申し出に最初に名乗り出た志願者の一部だった。二人は Albrecht の時代の Helminth の血清を注入され、部分的な Warframe に変えられた。Arthur の場合は Excalibur だった。体は Void に適応し、精神はそのまま保たれた。

変わり果てた志願者たちからは、Albrecht 本人の手で繰り返しサンプルが採られ、Arthur と Aoi の体の研究は Albrecht の研究所の Netracell の黒板に書き残されている。Albrecht 自身、彼らの人間性は長くはもたず、いずれ Helminth に丸ごと飲み込まれるのは時間の問題かもしれないとほのめかしている。

##### Whispers in the Walls

Arthur が初めて登場するのは、Tenno が Sanctum Anatomica で Albrecht の器に転移したときだ。Loid は、Murmur が転移の流れを器ではなく別の場所へそらしていると気づく。その結果、Tenno は Arthur の心の中に入る。

Arthur は、放棄された地下鉄で、1990 年代のコンピューターに取り憑いた Infested の人型 Techrot と戦う。彼は相棒の Aoi に連絡し、誰かが頭の中にいる気がすると言う。Aoi は、端末に接続して Dr. Entrati という男の居場所を突き止めるよう指示する。コンピューターにつなぐと、Aoi が攻撃を受けていると告げ、Mag の Crush の音が聞こえる。その後 Arthur も先ほどの怪物たちに襲われ、食い止めたところで気を失う。

目を覚ました Arthur の前に、博士と彼の Kavat が現れ、壁の中の者の Void 語が聞こえるなか、不気味に笑う。

##### The Hex

Höllvania Central Mall でくつろいでいた Arthur の手が突然 Void のエネルギーに乗っ取られ、「UNDR MIRAS AVE 10 MIN DNT B L8（Miras 通りの下、10 分、遅れるな）」と打ち込む。これを Dr. Entrati の手がかりと見た Arthur は、The Hex の面々（Amir、Aoi、Quincy、Leticia、Eleanor）に Atomicycle の鍵のありかを問いただす。出発する前に仲間たちが現れ、一人で手がかりを追わせはしないと言う。Arthur は Amir、Aoi、Quincy を援護につけ、Leticia と Eleanor に留守を任せる。

Höllvania の街へ走り出た Arthur は、見つけ次第撃てと部下に命じている Scaldra の指揮官 Major Neci Rusalka と遭遇する。Scaldra の軍と H-04 Efervon Tank を突破した後、Arthur は気を失うが、すぐに Eleanor からのテレパシーで、Entrati と彼の Kavat Kalymos が近くにいると知らされる。Kalymos は Techrot の洞窟の近くで姿を消す。中に入った Arthur の前で Helminth が Excalibur の Warframe を吐き出し、二人は Exalted Blade で激突する。そこへ Drifter が Excalibur から現れて Arthur に転移し、Arthur は Lotus、無関心、そして 1999 年の終わりに Höllvania の原子炉が爆発する幻を見る。Arthur は Drifter を追い出し、その手を刺す。

Arthur は Drifter を捕らえて尋問し、情報が嘘なら Techrot の餌にすると脅すが、時間がないことを認め、Entrati の捜索の先陣を切らせる。探し回った末に The Hex と Drifter は Entrati を見つけるが、Entrati と Drifter は無関心に連れ去られてしまう。

自分たちで核爆発を止めようと決めた Arthur、Amir、Aoi は原子炉の制御室に突入する。しかし The Hex は爆発を止められず、メンバーは一人ずつ死んでいく。Arthur は炉心に降りてくる Techrot の Babau と戦い、放射線に冒されて死ぬ。Drifter は The Hex 全員を救うと決意し、The Hex と親しくなれという Entrati の謎めいた警告に従って、1999 年の初めまで時間を巻き戻す。

> 「いいから……行け」（Arthur の最期）

###### Finale

> 「ねえ、ここから出よう、Arthur。二人で。いい？」（Drifter）

Arthur を放射線から救うため、Drifter は Arthur に転移し、彼の体を操って炉心から脱出させる。

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging（KIM）

Arthur のユーザー名は「Broadsword」。最初の何回かのやり取りではよそよそしく、過去の話を嫌がる。リーダーとしての役割を真剣に受け止める生真面目な人物として描かれる。On-lyne のファンではなく、Duviri での Drifter の生い立ちに興味を持っている。

全体的に生真面目でよそよそしいが、The Hex を深く気にかけ、みんなが無事か様子を確かめる。やがてそれは Drifter にも及び、十分に親しくなると、Drifter がトラウマを乗り越える手助けとして Duviri の話を全部聞かせてほしいと頼む。Drifter が Amir に悪夢のことを Arthur に話すよう勧めると、Arthur は Amir を慰め、自分の戦争の経験を分かち合って助ける。Drifter が他の Hex のメンバーと恋仲になると、Arthur は祝福する。

Drifter と付き合い始めると、その思いやりはいっそう強くなるが、同時に、深く関わることへのためらいや恐れも見えてくる。過去の経験のせいか、あるいは他の Hex の誰より（似た姿勢の Eleanor ほどではないにせよ）、親しい人をいずれ何らかの形で失うと覚悟し、予期しているからかもしれない。あるチャットでは、率直な話を装って、Drifter が 1999 年から永遠に去るつもりなのかと攻撃的に問い詰める。Drifter がそれを指摘すると、Arthur は謝り、Drifter が死ぬ悪夢を見ること、失うと考えるだけで苦しくてたまらず、心が取り返しのつかないほど傷つく前に遠ざけようと言い訳を探していたことを認める。それでも後に、たとえ Arthur と The Hex を置いていくことになっても Drifter は正しいことをするだろうと認め、死なないよう努めるか、自分を連れて行く方法を見つけると約束してくれと頼む。「Isleweaver」の後、Lizzie と終わりのない時間のループについて話した Arthur は、Drifter が新しいループで別の誰かを選ぶかもしれないという考えとも折り合いをつける。Drifter が Arthur と付き合いながら The Devil's Triad のメンバーと関係を始めると、Arthur の同意のチャットは、プレイヤーが選べば別れにつながる唯一のもので、別れても彼はさほど動揺していないように見える。

生真面目な性格のせいでくつろぐことができず、それは The Hex の今の状況で悪化していて、子ども時代にまでさかのぼる。仲間からもそれを指摘されている。後の会話では、以前より人生を楽しめるようになり、Eleanor が言うように再び歌い始めたことがわかる。仲間が「Hide_and_Seek」をして遊んでいるのを見たときも、仲間が役に立つことをしていないからではなく、誘われなかったことの方が不満だったと認める（前者のふりをしなければならなかった）。とはいえ、歌ったり遊んだりするのはまだ少し恥ずかしいと認め、Drifter はまたやってみるよう励ませる。

任務で見つけた「Sebastian」という名のテディベアを持っていて、時々話しかけている。

Arthur と Aoi はかつて付き合っていたが、Venusia（Star Days）に 1000 羽の折り鶴を贈られて関心を示さなかったため、Aoi に別れを告げられた。Eleanor によれば、二人の望みも正反対だった（Arthur はいずれ「外にバラの咲く小さな家」に落ち着き、自分のバーを開きたい。Aoi はもっと自由に人生を楽しみたい）。それでも友人としても仲間としても良い関係を保ち、Arthur は、彼女がいなければ The Hex、とりわけ自分はとっくに死んでいただろうと Aoi を深く尊敬している（Drifter との恋愛で互いが見せる反応は Aoi の項目を参照）。

Arthur と Eleanor は、かつてとても仲の良い姉弟だった。しかしある任務で Arthur の働きが目に見えて落ちた（Aoi との関係が終わった後だと強くほのめかされている）。言い争いになったとき、Eleanor は、彼が Aoi のことを考えているときはわかると言い返した。Eleanor は Arthur が信頼できる数少ない一人だったので、彼は「崩れ落ちた」。そのため今でも明らかに彼女を大切に思っているのに、信頼を壊されたことと彼女のテレパシーのせいで、話すのがとても難しくなっている。Drifter は、テレパシーを避けるため KIM で話してみてはと Arthur に勧め、関係の修復を手助けできる（Arthur が感謝するチャットもある）。Drifter が Eleanor に惹かれているとほのめかすと、最初は彼女をかばって身構えるが、Drifter が実際に彼女と付き合えば、彼女に安らぎを与えられる人が見つかったと喜んで認める。今の境遇にとっても、かつて婚約者の Christopher を戦争で失ったことにとっても。

酒好きでもあり、本人や他のメンバーのチャットでたびたび触れられる。落ち込んでいてすっかり酔っ払っている Arthur とのチャットもある。Backroom に来ると、モールに蒸留所を開きたいと言う。

料理も好きで、趣味として Drifter に教えようと申し出たり、Drifter のために料理したりする。あるチャットでは、Drifter のために四角いスパゲッティを作ろうとして見事に失敗する。Arthur は謝り、それからは普通の料理を作ると申し出るが、Drifter は後の会話でこの件をネタに彼をからかえる。Velimir が Techrot のバーベキューを考えたときも、Drifter は四角いスパゲッティの仕返しにサイコロ状に切って Arthur に食べさせようと提案でき、Velimir はそれが二人の内輪ネタだと気づく。Fables & Frontiers に誘われると、Amir によれば Arthur は仲間のためにお菓子を作る。

後には、趣味が似ていることから Velimir とかなり親しくなり、Eleanor によれば Velimir はある程度 Arthur の父親代わりになっている。Arthur が選べる立場にあったら、Velimir こそ選んだであろう父親だと彼女は言う。

#### トリビア

- Excalibur にちなみ、名前は聖剣エクスカリバーの使い手アーサー王から取られている。コードネームと KIM のユーザー名「Broadsword（大剣）」もそれを裏付ける。Lettie は皮肉を込めて彼を「勇敢なる王」と呼び、残りの Hex を円卓の騎士になぞらえている。Quincy は KIM で何度も「His Maj'（陛下）」と呼び、Amir も Fables & Frontiers について「厨房の王」と呼ぶ。
- Amir のチャットによれば、Arthur の傷は、力を得た直後の Amir が制御できずにいたとき、Amir を落ち着かせるため Arthur が自ら放電をまともに受けたことによる。左目はこのせいで重い白内障になっている。顔への落雷の長期的な後遺症として実際によくある症状。Sunder ヘルメットからのぞく Excalibur Umbra の左目と重なる。
- ある程度の PTSD を抱えているようで、今も戦争の悪夢を繰り返し見ている（Amir が Arthur と話すと明かされる）。
- 恋仲になると、ほのめかしを含んだ台詞が増え、Backroom に来るとさらに顕著になる。Quincy は、普段の堅い印象と違い、誰もいないときはそういう一面があるのかと Drifter に尋ねる。
- Quincy は、Arthur が Fables & Frontiers を遊ぶなら絶対にパラディンだと言う。実際に誘うと、Amir によればやはりパラディンを選ぶ。
- 初めて公開されたのは TennoCon 2023 の Whispers in the Walls のライブデモ。
- Quincy と Eleanor によれば、Arthur は歌がとても上手い。カラオケで酔ってマイクなしで英国の民謡「The Unquiet Grave」を歌ったことがある。Techrot Encore アップデート以降はモールで話しかけると一節を歌い、Minerva によれば Velimir と飲みながら「Soonerland」の第 1 幕を丸ごと歌ったこともある。

- 1999 では、The Hex の中で Arthur だけが Hex のワッペンの下に軍の階級章を付けている。ブリタニック陸軍の中尉だった過去を示すものだが、下向きの 2 本の山形は、現実の英国では中尉ではなく伍長の階級章にあたる。
- ドッグタグには 1999 の文字が刻まれていて、英字に直すと音で「arthur draehk」と読める。

#### Lore & History

##### Background

Originally a Britannic woman, Eleanor shared an idyllic childhood with Arthur, never fighting once. Eleanor would often braid Arthur's hair and use makeup on him.

Eleanor and Arthur's grandmother, Callista Penrose Nightingale, came to live with them due to "Pelham's Syndrome", which caused her mental state to deteriorate over time, to the point where she would be "shrieking" for long periods of time, even for the duration of their school day. Their father would make mention of putting a pillow over her head, leading Eleanor to suspect that he actually did kill their grandmother when she suddenly stopped shrieking in the middle of the night.

Prior to coming to Höllvania, Eleanor worked as a journalist. Despite her profession's habit of inflating stories for profit, she wanted to do some good with her work. After Arthur arrived in Höllvania and informed Eleanor of the events in the city, she felt a strong need to report on the Techrot infestation and inform the world of what had happened. She contacted a man named Danny MacIlvoy, obtained a forged visa and passport, and came into the city illegally. Due to the chaos within the city, she was able to slip in without incident. Eventually, she snuck into the subways, where she found an example of the Infestation - which she dubbed "Techrot" in her article on it. This would eventually replace the original name of the infection (Testudo Syndrome) and its scientific name (Technocyte Virus). Eventually, she became infected by the Techrot and sought a cure from Dr. Entrati.

##### Protoframe Experiments

Main article: Fragments/Albrecht

Before the events of 1999, the first volunteers that came forward to Dr. Entrati's offer to receive more than healing. They were then infused with a Helminth serum from Albrecht's time, which in turn morphed and alchemized them into partial Warframes. Their bodies became newly attuned to the Void, whilst their minds were kept intact.

Repeated samples were being taken from the now-transformed volunteers by Albrecht himself, with research of Aoi and Arthur's own anatomy written along the blackboards of the Netracells of Albrecht's Laboratories. It was implied by the doctor himself that their humanity may not last, and that it may be only a matter of time until the Helminth finally swallows them whole.

Eleanor's infection is the most advanced, to the point of having a large Infested tongue, and hearing the voice of the Techrot's group consciousness, trying to entice her to become a Legacyte.

##### The Hex

In Höllvania Central Mall, Eleanor is laying on the couch while overlooked by Lettie when they are approached by Arthur for his Atomicycle keys, which Eleanor gives. When Arthur attempts to ride out, Eleanor and the rest of The Hex show up and refuse to allow Arthur to investigate the lead on his own. Arthur instructs Amir, Aoi, and Quincy on backup while Lettie and Eleanor hold the fort. After Arthur is knocked unconscious following a battle with an H-04 Efervon Tank, Eleanor telepathically warns him that Dr. Entrati and his pet Kalymos are nearby.

When Arthur brings back the Drifter, who warns them that Höllvania's nuclear reactor will explode at the end of 1999, the Hex don't buy it but Arthur has them take point to search for Entrati.

After much searching, the Hex and the Drifter finally find Entrati, but Entrati and the Drifter are then taken away by the Indifference.

Deciding to stop the nuclear explosion themselves, Eleanor attempts to gain control of the Techrot by killing a Legacyte hivequeen. Eleanor leads the Techrot to the tunnels to breach the reactor access as Lettie guards her. However, as they manage to breach the lower level, Eleanor eventually succumbs to the Techrot's influence and attacks Lettie. Dialogue from KIM implies that the two managed to kill each other, with Eleanor tearing out Lettie's throat and Lettie managing to land a shot in Eleanor's chest. The Drifter resolves to save all of the Hex and rewinds time to the beginning of 1999, following a cryptic warning from Entrati to befriend the Hex.

> “Lettie: Eleanor!

> Eleanor: Shoot us in the back, Lettie? Lettie: Making sure that you are still... you. Eleanor: Of course, we are. And you can be, too.” —Lettie and Eleanor's final moments

###### Finale

> “Eleanor, only here to help. It's up to you.”

> —Drifter

The Drifter reminds Eleanor that she has aid from the Hex and the Drifter, preventing her from succumbing to the Techrot hivemind as she rips out her Infested tongue, which in turn saves Lettie.

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Eleanor

Eleanor's username is "Salem". Originally possessing a career in journalism, she enjoys long extended conversations with the Drifter, though can be sometimes playful in unexpected ways. However, she also displays moments of vulnerability due to being more deeply infected than the rest of The Hex, fearing that others will see her as a monster.

She is often philosophical in her chats with the Drifter, questioning them on how they interpret death and the possibility of how a personality or "soul" survives after death, additionally asking them about their nature as one of the two known versions of the Tenno, coming to the conclusion that she might also have another version of herself who became a nun. She also appreciates it when the Drifter is legitimately concerned for her, as the rest of the Hex usually only check on her to keep track on the growth of her Infestation.

She is also rather overt, treating sexual contact rather casually and acknowledges it as a good morale booster. If the Drifter is good enough with their words, she will make subtle flirts towards them and, if she accepts to date the Drifter through the KIM chat, she will teasingly "suggest" they have sexual intercourse in Arthur's office with the mic on as a way to let the Hex (and particularly Lettie) know they are officially a couple. If the Drifter starts a relationship with a member of the Devil's Triad while already in a relationship with her, she will accept without thinking too much about it (Having read The Drifter's mind before they even mention this through KIM) and bemoan not being able to join them directly while also teasingly suggesting she enjoys the experience through the Drifter.

Both Aoi and Eleanor have mentioned that the latter attempted to leave the Hex in the dead of night after a particularly dark day during a raid due to their fear of her Infestation causing her to fully turn, with the intention of helping them from a distance. Thanks to Aoi closing down the garage's security gates and talking to her, she was able to convince Eleanor to stay.

She holds a degree of fear that the Hex will eventually turn on her. She is especially afraid that Arthur may kill her as an act of mercy, as she suspects their father killed their grandmother due to her mental illness and has recurring nightmares of him mercy killing her. She will eventually ask the Drifter if they would kill her. Depending on the Drifter's reasoning, she will either respond in a neutral fashion if they reason out why they would kill her or in a happily surprised fashion if they reply that they would not kill her from the start, with the latter leading into an additional conversation confirming the Drifter's stance. Depending on The Drifter's choices, they might be able to confess their feelings if they desire to romance Eleanor.

Eleanor describes to Drifter the one time she tapped into the mind of Rusalka during The Hex quest, and found that her mind had been hollowed out, hiding a vast black where she accidentally exposed herself to The Indifference. Though she did not see his physical form, it nevertheless was aware of her, recognizing her existence yet remaining indifferent towards her. This crushed Eleanor's spirits, and while she was not driven mad, the knowledge caused for her an existential crisis. The Drifter can opt to say that while they do not know if the Indifference will ultimately win, they do not want them to win at that moment, or they can attempt to encourage Eleanor to fight against the Indifference. Eleanor will react positively to the former, appreciating the Drifter's attempts to comfort her, or react angrily to the latter, asking if all the Drifter and Arthur can understand is fighting. This is eventually resolved when Eleanor is able to traverse the Void to meet her other "nun" self, despite the Indifference attempting to follow her - knowing that one version of her has taken the nun path, she no longer regrets taking her path as a Protoframe.

She can also share a humorous telepathic encounter she had with one of Lettie's rats. She ended up somewhat jealous of the simple mindset of the rat, but also had to sever their connection quickly as to avoid overwhelming it.

Lettie holds a degree of animosity towards Eleanor, due to Eleanor's ability to read minds. Eleanor hypothesizes that Lettie does not want her to see whatever secrets she hides, and that Lettie may be attracted to her, though she ultimately does not care. Another reason for this is due to Eleanor's sadistic mindset when it comes to dealing with her enemies, clearly enjoying killing and causing pain to them. She also enjoys hearing about The Drifter's tales of conquest over the Orokin, all of which leaves Lettie feeling as though the former enjoys having gone through Albrecht's experiments. That said, she reserves this sadistic attitude for her enemies only and, in fact, she is very empathetic towards the people she cares about (even when she likes to mess with them from time to time) while mostly hiding how she really feels about being turned into a Protoframe. As an example, she helps Amir extinguish his anxiety episodes and while she doesn't think too much of Lettie despite being former friends, she still cares about her.

Eleanor once had a fiance named Christopher, who was also Arthur's best friend and would-be commanding officer in the Crimson Watch. However, Christopher would die due to a roadside bomb. This tragedy would cause Eleanor to resent the idea of "heroes," and both she and Arthur would come together to mourn his death on its anniversary. This resentment of heroes is shown as one of her personal beliefs in a "Hero Virus" (which she seems to liken to a savior complex), which she believes Arthur (and by extension Christopher) has to an extreme degree.

Eleanor's powers as a Protoframe based on Nyx allow her to passively sense strong emotions and internal dialogues, focus in on certain minds from a distance, and directly communicate with her targets through telepathy. However, when she initially received her powers, she sensed the natural biological urges of six other people, which her body assumed was actually hers, leading her to suffer what she initially believed to be a bladder infection. She also implies that she can sense the Hex's thoughts at all times, but she deliberately leaves it ambiguous to them whether or not she hears their particular thoughts.

She later reveals this is a result of an incident between her and Arthur: during a mission, Arthur was noticeably underperforming (heavily implied to be after his relationship with Aoi fell apart). When this caused an argument, Eleanor retorted that she could tell when he was thinking about Aoi, leading him to "crumple" given that Eleanor was one of the few people he did trust. Eleanor elaborates that, with her powers, she can virtually win arguments or altercations from the start by digging up whatever the person's weakness is from their mind, and waving it around in their face, something that she feels guilty about. That being said, Eleanor still clearly cares about Arthur despite her overall attitude showing otherwise as she admits to the Drifter she misses how close they used to be as siblings and, should the Drifter hint at romantically pursuing Arthur, she will immediately become protective of him if to the point of threatening to murder them if they ever hurt Arthur on purpose.

Given her "latent infection" of Techrot, she possesses a stronger connection to the hivemind than the other Hex. She is considered a possible queen and rival to other Legacytes, and she can mention that the hivemind's consciousness occasionally reaches out to her in dreams in an attempt to draw her in.

Kaya later describes that conversations with Eleanor are a "minefield", remarking she tends to have very explosive reactions when talking and as a result never knows how the latter will react. The Drifter can reassure Eleanor she is not a minefield or call her out on this, to which she admits she has become overly reliant on physical expressions and her psychic abilities to read the emotions of the people she talks to, both of which are useless over KIM, which has the result of assuming the worst of what she's reading. That said, she apologizes for her behaviour while also promising to reflect on her past conversations with the Drifter and be more careful in the future since, regardless of this, The Drifter has never backed down away from her.

#### Trivia

- Her status as Arthur's sibling is a reference to Nyx's pre-alpha concept design as a female Excalibur.
- Though she and Arthur are twins, they have different birthdays, as Eleanor was born at a minute before midnight and Arthur was born two minutes after her, one minute into the next day.
- Her KIM username, "Salem", likely refers to the Massachusetts town Salem, where the Salem Witch Trials took place. This is also referenced by Lettie, who calls Eleanor "bruja" (Spanish for "witch") in her chats.
- Quincy calls her a "war journo" in one of his chats, suggesting Eleanor may have specifically been a war correspondent and not just a regular journalist. However, since this is the only instance where she is referred to this way and Eleanor's focus as a journalist appears to have been whistleblowing or otherwise digging up secrets, it's unknown if this is actually the case.
- Her being deeply infected with the Technocyte is likely a reference to Nemesis from Digital Extremes' earlier title, Dark Sector, who served as a major antagonist heavily infected by the Technocyte Virus and was the design inspiration for the Nyx Nemesis Skin.
- Under most circumstances, Eleanor does not speak out loud, instead using telepathy to communicate with those around her. While using her telepathy, her eyes flash a brighter purple. While possessed by the Techrot hivemind during the events of The Hex, her eyes instead glow yellow/green.

Eleanor's tongue has been mutated into a large Techrot tentacle, preventing her from speaking normally due to its size. It heals quickly and grows back if severed.

  - While it does not grow any longer, the tongue gradually puts out "frondy bits" which Eleanor shaves off to keep them from growing too long. Comparing it to crab meat, she once tried to make burgers out of the clippings, much to the disgust of the rest of the Hex.
  - She is able to speak normally (if slightly strained) for a brief moment after ripping her tongue out near the end of The Hex Finale. However, the usage of her telepathy at the end of the Finale suggests it grew back near-instantly.
  - She is later heard sending a message during Amir's Shockwave, although how exactly is never specified. However, Amir's reaction beforehand and Eleanor sounding slightly strained at the beggining of it might suggest she just ripped her tongue again to do so.
- She seems to have metallic canine teeth instead of regular ones and the sclera in her eyes has become completely black, likely as a result of her transformation into a protoframe.

According to her dialogue in Tennocon 2024's Höllvania Central Mall relay, she is aware of the Tenno's presence (as the player's model became translucent when near an NPC, suggesting the Hex cannot actually perceive them) and promises to keep them a secret from the other members of the Hex.

  - One of her comments regards "something bursting" and "crossed swords", foreshadowing the clash between Arthur and the Drifter during The Hex quest and suggesting Eleanor possesses the ability of foresight.
- She envies Aoi, believing their powers should have been switched so that Aoi could "spread happiness through everyone's heads", and Eleanor would "throw cars and trucks around".

She seems to be able to induce perceptual phenomena like Synesthesia in other people to a certain degree, as indicated by one of Aoi's voicelines.

  - This is shown to be part of her telepathy as one of her chats mentions she is able to "broadcast" a better mood to the civilians in the Mall.
- Pelham's Syndrome seems to be somewhat related to the real life condition of dementia. This is supported by the Drifter who mentions Archimedean Suda having a similar condition before being turned into a Cephalon.

She has some liking for art and painting as well as literature, and will sometimes recite verses from poems and plays. Some examples are:

  - "If ever two were one, then surely we" - Anne Bradstreet's To My Dear and Loving Husband
  - "I carry you still on my bloodstream’s current" - One of many possible translations of Rainer Maria Rilke's Lösch mir die Augen aus
  - "Once more unto the breach" - William Shakespeare's Henry V (Act III, Scene I) - "Once more unto the breach, dear friends, once more".
- She also has a fondness for plants, with one of her liked gifts being a hanging planter and Lettie wanting to build a greenhouse partly to make Eleanor smile.
- While discussing Fables & Frontiers, Amir contemplates asking Eleanor to be the game's "Fablemaster", the equivalent of Dungeons & Dragons' Dungeon Master, as he feels she would be perfect for the role. This is an allusion to Amelia Tyler's previous work as the narrator of Baldur's Gate 3.
- In one of her KIM conversations regarding her attempt to leave The Hex and support the team from a distance, she mentions looking at herself at the mirror and imagining her reflection grabbing her by the throat and blaming her for the current situation after a raid, with the Hex also being in a darker mood than usual (such as Arthur and Aoi not talking to each other). This is partially shown in "The Hex - Warframe: 1999 Animated Prologue".
- Eleanor is the only member of the Hex who has no conversations locked behind dating her. She is also the only romanceable protoframe whose consent conversation cannot be skipped if the Drifter starts a relationship with a member of The Devil's Triad while already in a relationship with her.
- One of Eleanor's Rank 6 conversations, where she asks the Drifter if she is "a minefield", references common player criticism of Eleanor's habit of overreacting and immediately logging off KIM in response to seemingly-harmless dialogue choices from the player.
- When joining the player in Höllvania bounties, Eleanor will sometimes use Absorb as normal, and sometimes as if Assimilate was present. The reason for this is unknown.

#### 経歴

##### Protoframe の実験

Arthur と Aoi は、治療以上のものを受けるという Dr. Entrati の申し出に最初に名乗り出た志願者の一部だった。二人は Albrecht の時代の Helminth の血清を注入され、部分的な Warframe に変えられた。Aoi の場合は Mag だった。体は Void に適応し、精神はそのまま保たれた。

変わり果てた志願者たちからは、Albrecht 本人の手で繰り返しサンプルが採られ、Arthur と Aoi の体の研究は Albrecht の研究所の Netracell の黒板に書き残されている。Albrecht 自身、彼らの人間性は長くはもたず、いずれ Helminth に丸ごと飲み込まれるのは時間の問題かもしれないとほのめかしている。

> 「このクソみたいな一年もあと少し。やり直せたらいいのに。初日にあの Dr. Entrati の野郎を見つけられたら、大晦日を一人で過ごさなくて済んだのに」

##### The Hex

Höllvania Central Mall でトランプタワーを作っていた Aoi は、Arthur に Atomicycle の鍵を求められるが、直結のやり方くらい覚えろとあしらう。Arthur が一人で出て行こうとすると、Aoi たち The Hex が現れ、一人で手がかりを追わせはしないと言う。Arthur は Aoi、Amir、Quincy を援護につけ、Leticia と Eleanor に留守を任せる。

Arthur が連れ帰った Drifter が、1999 年の終わりに Höllvania の原子炉が爆発すると警告しても、The Hex は信じない。それでも Arthur は Dr. Entrati の捜索の先陣を切らせる。Aoi は Drifter に Atomicycle の鍵を渡し、Arthur を傷つけたらただじゃおかないと釘を刺し、塗装に傷をつけるなと言う。

一行は Entrati が Scaldra の車列に連れ去られるのを見つけるが、Aoi はトラックに押さえつけられて動けなくなる。Drifter に助けられた Aoi は、目立たないトラックを使って車列に近づくことにする。The Hex と Drifter はついに Entrati にたどり着くが、Entrati と Drifter は無関心に連れ去られてしまう。

自分たちで核爆発を止めようと決めた Arthur、Amir、Aoi は原子炉の制御室に突入する。Aoi が制御棒を固定しようとすると、棒が外れてしまう。The Hex は爆発を止められず、Aoi は力を使い果たして死ぬ。Drifter は The Hex 全員を救うと決意し、The Hex と親しくなれという Entrati の謎めいた警告に従って、1999 年の初めまで時間を巻き戻す。

###### Finale

> 「できる。あなたは強いから」（Drifter）

Drifter に励まされた Aoi は、制御棒を固定していた機構を壊し、手で棒を差し込む。

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging（KIM）

Aoi のユーザー名は「xX GLIMMER Xx」。On-lyne の大ファンで、特に Zeke が好きで彼をアバターにしている。The Hex の整備士として、メンバーの Atomicycle を定期的に整備している。東洋系らしく、チャットでは顔文字をよく使う。大切な人々（The Hex など）を守ろうとする本能があり、彼らを傷つけようとする者には暴力も辞さないと脅す（最初は信用していなかった Drifter も脅した）。親しくなると Drifter もその対象になり、恋人になるとさらに強まる（Gemini の台詞にも表れている）。The Hex の面々からはチームの心臓と見なされ、本人たちも彼女が一番いい人だと認めている。

学者の家系の出で、父はロケットを手がける航空宇宙エンジニア、母は宇宙システム設計の教師で、二人は大学の学生時代に出会った。ただ、両親はいつも忙しく、Aoi が育つ間あまり一緒に過ごせなかったという。そのため Aoi が兵士になると決めたとき、両親はそれが彼女に望んでいた生き方ではないとはっきり伝えた。それでも彼女は両親を恨んではおらず、今も良好な関係にあるらしい。

The Hex や Drifter と出会う前、Aoi はカナタの大都市の一つで育ち、Arthur とともに国際危機対応（ICR）部隊に所属していて、任務の一環で Höllvania に送られた。ICR に入ったのは、自分の信じるもののために戦い、人々を守るためで、後悔はしていないという。

とても人懐っこいが、バカだとか浅はかだと思われるのは嫌い。ICR にいた頃は部隊の「天然」扱いだったと語り、それを隠れた強みだと考えている。そのおかげで、他の人なら許されないことも見逃してもらえるからだ。とはいえ真剣な話題になると態度も真剣になり、チャットでは句読点をきちんと使い、顔文字をまったく使わなくなる。

ゲームも好きで、Fables & Frontiers に誘われると、スクラップの金属で Amir のために新しいフィギュアを作る。脚に付けているキーホルダーは昔手に入れた JINYMON のキャラクターで、最初の 3 体のうちの 1 体。JINYMON のカードも集めているが、コレクションは Amir ほど大きくない。

Aoi と Arthur はかつて付き合っていた。しかし Venusia（Star Days）に 1000 羽の折り鶴を贈ったとき Arthur が関心を示さなかったため、Aoi は別れを告げた。Eleanor によれば、二人の望みも正反対だった。Arthur はどこかに落ち着き（「外にバラの咲く小さな家」）、自分のバーを開きたいと願い、Aoi はもっと自由に人生の楽しみを味わいたいと願っている。それでも友人としても仲間としても良い関係を保っていて、Arthur は、彼女がいなければ The Hex、とりわけ自分はとっくに死んでいただろうと Aoi を深く尊敬している。Drifter が Arthur と恋仲になると、Aoi は Backroom に戻る彼に微笑んで賛成を示し、Arthur も彼女にもいつか誰かが見つかるよう願っていると語る。逆に Drifter が Aoi と恋仲になると、Arthur は嫉妬はしないが、自分にはできなかった形で Drifter が Aoi を幸せにできることを少し寂しく思うと認め、それでも彼女が幸せを見つけて嬉しいから、できれば傷つけないでくれと頼む。

チャットでは、ピアノが弾けて Drifter に教えようかと申し出ることもある。

#### 人物

##### Protoframe 調書

#### 経歴

##### 生い立ち

Amir は子どもの頃からプログラミングをしていて、COBOL、C++、Python（現実の言語）に通じている。リベルタティア合衆国出身で、はじめは Höllvania の原子炉で情報セキュリティのインターンとして働いていた。そこへ Albrecht Entrati が彼のコンピューターをハッキングして、原子炉の仕様を渡せと要求してきた。Amir が仕様を渡すと、雇い主は彼を国家安全保障上の脅威と見なし、Scaldra に追わせた。Amir は Entrati からポケベルで送られた住所へ向かい、Scaldra に見つかったら使えと小瓶を渡された。

やがて彼はその小瓶を使い、激痛に襲われた。錯乱状態の中で、追ってきた Scaldra の部隊を皆殺しにし、見つけてくれた Arthur にも怪我を負わせた。Arthur の顔の傷と片目の失明はこのときのものだ。

##### Protoframe の実験

Amir は The Hex の他の面々と同じく、Albrecht の時代の Helminth の血清を注入され、部分的な Warframe に変えられた。Amir の場合は Volt だった。体は Void に適応し、精神はそのまま保たれた。

変わり果てた志願者たちからは、Albrecht 本人の手で繰り返しサンプルが採られた。Albrecht 自身、彼らの人間性は長くはもたず、いずれ Helminth に丸ごと飲み込まれるのは時間の問題かもしれないとほのめかしている。

##### The Hex

Höllvania Central Mall でアーケードゲームをしていた Amir は、Arthur に Atomicycle の鍵を求められるが無視する。Arthur が一人で出て行こうとすると、Amir たち The Hex が現れ、一人で手がかりを追わせはしないと言う。Arthur は Amir、Aoi、Quincy を援護につけ、Leticia と Eleanor に留守を任せる。

Arthur が Drifter を連れ帰ると、Amir は Drifter を「Marty McFlea」と呼び、このあだ名が The Hex に定着する。Drifter が 1999 年の終わりに Höllvania の原子炉が爆発すると警告したとき、核の話を信じたのは Amir だけだった。ハッカーとして Y2K 問題を知っていたからだ。

Dr. Entrati を探し回った末に The Hex と Drifter はついに彼を見つけるが、Entrati と Drifter は無関心に連れ去られてしまう。Amir は、Marty の話は本当だったと言う。

自分たちで核爆発を止めようと決めた Arthur、Amir、Aoi は原子炉の制御室に突入し、Amir は原子炉にハッキングを仕掛ける。しかし The Hex は爆発を止められず、Amir はパニック発作を起こした末に Entrati に撃ち殺される。Drifter は The Hex 全員を救うと決意し、The Hex と親しくなれという Entrati の謎めいた警告に従って、1999 年の初めまで時間を巻き戻す。

> 「俺……遅すぎた」（Amir の最期）

###### Finale

> 「Amir、ゆっくり。（深呼吸）手を出して」（Drifter）

パニック発作を起こした Amir に、Drifter はゆっくり深呼吸して手を出すよう言う。そこに現れた Parazon で、ハッキングは完了する。

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging（KIM）

Amir のユーザー名は「H16h V0l7463」（High Voltage）。スピード自慢らしく打つのがとても速く、1 秒の間に何度も返信してくることが多く、そのぶん少し注意散漫。ロボット、テクノロジー、ゲームの大ファンで、その話題になると目を輝かせて質問攻めにする。ユーモアのセンスはあるが、からかわれるのは嫌い。

Amir に言わせれば、「ハッキング」は実際よりずっと派手に描かれすぎていて、ほとんどはコードを書くこととパスワードを当てることだ。チャットでは、Finale の前まで自分が Parazon を持っていることも使い方も知らなかったと明かされる。だから The Hex のクエストでは Parazon を使えず、以前のセキュリティ権限もないので、超高速でパスワードを打ち込んで原子炉の端末に入ろうとしていたのだ。とはいえ、とても小さい頃からコードを書いていて、綴りを覚えるより先にタイピングを覚えたと認めている。

Amir は ADHD（注意欠如・多動症）を抱えていて、自己肯定感が低い。そのため自分を「直す」手段として Cephalon に興味を持つが、Drifter から Cephalon の負の面（強制された服従、不完全さ、罰として不死にされた存在であること）を聞いて、その考えを捨てる。

暇なときにはビデオゲームを作っていて、Drifter に意見を聞くこともあるが、時々自分の意見で押し切る。The Hex で Fables & Frontiers を遊びたいとも思っているが、「オタクっぽすぎる」と思われていて、みんな忙しいので断られるだろうとわかっている。Drifter が 2 人以上を説得できると、すぐにキャンペーンを書き始める。その後、他のメンバーも渋っていたわりに楽しんでいると喜び、Drifter に感謝する。KIM で何人説得できたかにかかわらず、最終的には全員でキャンペーンを始める。

ずっと後、退屈して在庫確認をさぼりたくなった Amir は、牢屋からの脱出を描いたテキストアドベンチャーを Drifter にテストプレイさせる。Grineer、Lotus への言及、巨大ネズミ（Lettie には見せるつもりがない）、Amir のオリジナルキャラクター Zabir（どんでん返しとして Lotus は悪で自分を閉じ込めたと主張する）が登場し、書けなくなったところで Grineer に小惑星基地があると知って、最後は Railjack で脱出する。Drifter が謎解きで詰まるとヒントをくれるが、無意味なことをして無限ループさせようとすると苛立ち、古代の神に殺させる。それでももう一度チャンスはくれる。最後に、実は在庫確認はもう終わっていたと明かす。

負けず嫌いでもある。Drifter が Caliber Chicks 2 を遊んだことがあると、自分のハイスコアを破ろうとしているのかと言ってくる。「Lonestar Hold'Em」など他のゲームや Faceoff でも同じだ。

軍の経験がある他のメンバー（Arthur、Aoi、Quincy、Lettie は軍務経験者、Eleanor は記者）と違い、Amir は民間人の出で戦闘経験がない。そのため戦いの心の傷がずっと深く、原子炉のハッキング中のようなパニック発作や、繰り返す悪夢に苦しんでいる。特に、力を得て制御を失ったときの記憶の悪夢がある。そのとき Arthur が彼を落ち着かせるため放電をまともに受け、顔の傷と片目の失明を負った。Amir は最初 Drifter に当たり散らすが、後の会話で不安を打ち明ける。Drifter は、Arthur と Quincy に別々に話すよう勧めたり、直接慰めたりして、彼が向き合う手助けができる。

Amir にとって Quincy は「命を懸けられる」兄弟であり、同時に「刺したくなる」兄弟でもある。口喧嘩はしても、心から Quincy を大切に思っている。

Aoi によれば、Amir はかつて自分を他のメンバーの脇役だと思っていた。しかし、HitMaker が On-lyne を Technocyte Coda に置き換えようとしていることを Aoi とともに暴いてから、ずっと自信をつけた。

###### Fables & Frontiers

Drifter が The Hex と十分な信頼を築くと、Amir は Drifter を、中心メンバーとともにこのテーブルトーク RPG のセッションに誘う（上のキャンペーンの手伝いとは関係なく）。Amir は「Fablemaster」を務める。「現在」へのネタが満載のキャンペーンを始めるのに最初は大はしゃぎだったが、他のメンバーが Höllvania の通りに潜む「不審な目撃情報」の調査に呼ばれてキャンペーンが中断するたびに、だんだん皮肉っぽく引きこもりがちになる。

セッションの 6 日目、Amir が無関心に心を奪われていたことが強くほのめかされる。時間のループに閉じ込められた苛立ちを Drifter に向け、時代を越えて敵に力を与えていた。それが「目撃情報」の正体だった。The Hex の助けを借りて Drifter は Amir を落ち着かせ、Void の支配から解放する。Amir は最近の振る舞いを謝り、キャンペーン自体が無関心の影響下で作ったものだったと認め、自分がうっかり生み出した新たな脅威への対処を手伝うと決める。

#### Lore & History

##### Background

Lettie met Arthur while both were stationed in the same base, with her acting as the field medic of her unit in the Libertatian Army. With her and Arthur as the only survivors in battle, Lettie treated Arthur's wounds and joined the International Crisis Response (ICR) alongside him, Aoi, and Quincy.

She has a fondness for animals and owns four rats named Vaquero (cowboy), Anita (named after a friend back home), Neenah (a deceased friend), and Tláloc (an ancient god of rain).

##### Protoframe Experiments

Main article: Fragments/Albrecht

Lettie was one of the first volunteers that came forward to Dr. Entrati's offer to receive more than healing. They were then infused with a Helminth serum from Albrecht's time, which in turn morphed and alchemized them into partial Warframes. Their bodies became newly attuned to the Void, whilst their minds were kept intact.

Repeated samples were taken from the now-transformed volunteers by Albrecht himself. It was implied by the doctor himself that their humanity may not last, and that it may be only a matter of time until the Helminth finally swallows them whole.

##### The Hex

In Höllvania Central Mall, Lettie is overlooking Eleanor when they are approached by Arthur for his Atomicycle keys, which Eleanor gives. When Arthur attempts to ride out, Lettie and the rest of The Hex show up and refuse to allow Arthur to investigate the lead on his own. Arthur instructs Amir, Aoi, and Quincy on backup while Lettie and Eleanor hold the fort.

When Arthur brings back the Drifter for questioning, Lettie stitches their hand after it was stabbed by Arthur. When the Drifter warns them that Höllvania's nuclear reactor will explode at the end of 1999, the Hex don't buy it but Arthur has them take point to search for Dr. Entrati. Lettie later complains that the Hex should've had packed up and left Höllvania weeks ago.

After much searching, the Hex and the Drifter finally find Entrati, but Entrati and the Drifter are then taken away by the Indifference.

Deciding to stop the nuclear explosion themselves, Eleanor attempts to gain control of the Techrot with Lettie monitoring her to breach the reactor access. Lettie points her gun at Eleanor to ensure she is still sane and is convinced to lower her guard. However, the Hex ultimately fail to stop the explosion, as Eleanor succumbs to the Techrot hivemind and attacks Lettie. The Drifter resolves to save all of the Hex and rewinds time to the beginning of 1999, following a cryptic warning from Entrati to befriend the Hex.

#### Lore & History

At age 19, Kaya is the youngest of the Protoframes and is a self-taught genius inventor with an intense desire to learn about time travel.

She managed to track Albrecht Entrati all by herself and was fully aware that he was from the future, which caught him off-guard.

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Kaya

Kaya's username is "KOLTrial_5115", although she prefers to go by "Nova" online and notably lacks an avatar. She wastes absolutely no time getting to the point and prefers short and direct conversations, preferably topics that involve time travel. Despite being the youngest of the Protoframes, she despises being treated like a kid.

Coming off as standoffish, Kaya firmly does not trust the Drifter, to the point she declares to be using them to further her own goals. It is also shown she is very proud of her intelligence, as when The Drifter jokes she would not be able to bake a cake without having learned the process, she immediately stops what she's doing and goes to bake the cake out of spite.

She eventually reveals her obsession with time traveling, her anonymous KIM profile, and her trust issues all stem from being a victim of cyberbullying: she sent a nude photo of herself which was leaked over the internet, irreparably damaging her reputation, and seeks to travel to a future that has long forgotten this incident to move past this, and so people her age would respect her intelligence again. This appears to be the reason why her KIM profile seems to be a new, trial account which lacks any determinant characteristics, such as her real name or profile picture, suggesting this is the only way she can stay safe on the 1999-era internet. That said, she still has moments where she can allow herself to relax, as she mentions to The Drifter she collects JINYMON cards, collectible cards based on a show.

As a result of this, she will rebuff any attempts by the Drifter to flirt with her and thus she cannot be romanced. She will later mention that she has a crush on Arthur, also calling the Drifter "lucky" if they are dating him. Arthur later finds her following him around and becomes panicked and fearful of her powers upon learning this from The Drifter.

If the Drifter mentions Duviri and Argon Crystals when Kaya inquires about them, and then allow her to ramble when asked, she will make a breakthrough and use the Void as a trampoline to follow the Drifter into the future, leaving behind an automated message as she arrives at Larunda Relay on Mercury where she can be found sitting on the top balcony of the concourse. She will still manage the Temporal Archimedea missions regardless, showing she can regularly blink in and out of each time period, which is confirmed by Lettie in later chats.

Otherwise, she will accept being stuck in 1999 and not allow her regrets to define her before terminating her KIM account.

Regardless of the outcome, she eventually comes around to liking The Hex as a whole, helping in surgery and asking "creepy" medical questions to Lettie or startling Amir with her teleport abilities (much to Lettie's amusement), with Quincy also mentioning having a conversation about quantum physics with her while he was on watch. It is also shown that most of the members of the Hex have grown to care about her to some degree, with them agreeing that what was done to her was heinous. Lettie in particular seems to have become the closest to her, as she seems to relate to Kaya's difficulties growing up with her own. That said, they all agree to let her get close to them at her own pace.

#### Lore & History

Much like their namesake, Flare possesses a fiery rebellious spirit that is reflected in their rock 'n' roll music. They are the last member of their band, Temple and the Rippers, who were executed by the Scaldra.

Flare and their band came to Höllvania following an invitation from Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi, who claimed to be a fan and requested that they "help him in his noble cause" to set the city straight. The band accepted purely out of spite, but Flare got infected by Techrot prior to the performance and received Entrati's treatment. When their performance inspired the people of Höllvania to rebel, to the point of toppling statues and tipping over Scaldra trucks, Viktor retaliated by having the rest of the band executed on national television, leaving Flare - who was still struggling to adjust to their new abilities - the band's sole survivor.

Mourning the loss of their band and filled with rage, Flare shredded on their guitar until their hands bled, the infected blood seeping into the guitar which would create Lizzie.

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Flare

Flare's username is "Liminus_Star". They are deeply concerned about their infection and come to the Drifter in hopes of a cure, especially since their guitar, Lizzie, "threatens" to "make them one".

Lizzie, who eventually reveals herself to be a manifestation of the Helminth, claims that she "loves" Flare and pleads to the "demon" to convince Flare that she is not a threat. However, as part of the Helminth, her thought processes are very alien to Flare's concerns. If the Drifter continuously acts hostile towards Lizzie, she will occasionally jumpscare them with edited photos of Flare in combination with a guitar riff.

As part of the Helminth, Lizzie has a bizarre appetite for inedible objects like cardboard boxes and car tires, and consumes through Flare due to their symbiotic nature much to their disgust.

Due to Lizzie's highly possessive nature towards them, Flare cannot be romanced by the Drifter.

Flare will voice their issues with societal expectations and finding their purpose in life with the Drifter. The Drifter can then intuit that Flare's desire to be "a part of something greater" is something that Lizzie attempts to fix by joining Flare with the Infested Hivemind.

The Drifter can attempt to broker peace between Flare and Lizzie, convincing Flare to come to terms with her while persuading Lizzie that her idea of assimilation is driving Flare away, ultimately bringing them together with music. Additionally, if the Drifter accepts the offer to play Lizzie, Flare will deliver an inbox message containing the Lizzie Shawzin, while revealing that Flare discovered that they are the original Temple Warframe and with Kaya's help plans to hibernate on a comet until its round trip for The Collapse and ignite the flames of revolution.

However, if the Drifter tells Flare that they consider Lizzie insane, threatens to kill Lizzie and does not apologize for making an enemy out of her, or tells Lizzie that working together means nobody else can hurt them, negotiations will fail as Flare sees themselves as a lost cause while Lizzie calls them ignorant to their situation and demands they start over. The pair will then cease any further communications over KIM.

If the player has met Lizzie then chooses to 'forget' on starting a new loop of the 1999 Calendar, Lizzie reveals she is aware of this and remarks that they "like to shake the little magnetic toy and give things another go-around".

#### Lore & History

Unlike many royal Orokin, Roathe was a direct commander of Dax during The Old War. He is sometimes nicknamed the "Devil of Tau" due to his ruthless determination to win at all costs. It continued to be used as a title for him as he defected to Anarchs along with his lover Executor Nitokh, and also represented his unrepentant attitude towards his past, in addition to his strong dislike of the Orokin for their spoiling of Tau and for wasting the moral compromises he made in their name.

He is initially annoyed with the presence of Marie and Lyon, regarding them as as superstitious throwbacks to an earlier time before Void science and resenting both the fact that Albrecht Entrati assigned them as his wardens and that he requires their assistance and the Drifter's in uncovering his memories within The Descendia, though he acknowledges their necessity.

Roathe was brought to XX99 through a Helminth, which immediately withered and died on arrival. He was able to reverse-engineer one, albeit with significant difficulty due to lack of electricity and equipment, through his Infested blood from the Protoframe serum, to bring himself, Marie, and Lyon to the Sanctum Anatomica, where they now reside within a replica of the Cathedral's crypts.

##### Kinemantik Instant Messaging

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messenger/Roathe

Roathe is a very pragmatic individual, appreciating efficiency and practicality, as seen with his belief that the Indifference is not a "god" despite its immense powers. He does however enjoy philosophical debates, occasionally questioning the Drifter on their own views on certain aspects of life, such as the nature of "evil".

Roathe is unique among the Protoframes in that alongside direct messages, he will also leave voice messages to listen to on the POM-2. These messages detail his point of view of the Orokin Empire, the Old War, and part of the history of the Perita Rebellion. He leaves one voice message for each time he is defeated in the depths of The Descendia, for a total of twenty-one, though there is a cap to how many messages can be sent depending on conversation progress.

Roathe will offer to have Loid reunite with the Entrati upstairs in exchange for assistance recovering his memories within The Descendia. Should the Drifter follow through by defeating his boss counterpart 21 times (tracked by a counter called "Descendia Voicemails Unlocked" in Roathe's KIM notes), convince Marie and Lyon for their help, and reach Rank 5 - Family with the syndicate, Loid will finally agree to reveal himself to the family. Upon arriving at the Necralisk, a cutscene shows Loid exiting the hidden room, with Roathe physically blocking him when he gets cold feet and attempts to retreat; Roathe will also appear upstairs in front of Mother to replay the cutscene and offer Honoria for purchase.

Despite having initially requested the Drifter's assistance in restoring his memories, Roathe gradually comes to fear these memories and will ask the Drifter to stop their ventures within the Descendia. Depending on whether the Drifter follows his request or continues to uncover more of his memories, Roathe will either be pleased with the Drifter's inaction (although this is considered a 'failure' for his arc) or become distressed by the memories and ask for them to be wiped via a reset, regardless of the fact that doing so would affect Lyon and Marie as well.

Performing a reset (whether he has asked for one or not) has no effect on Roathe's HellStory counter, how many voice messages he has sent, or on Loid's reunion. However, if Roathe's HellStory counter is at 21 or Loid has been reunited with the Entrati family, then Roathe will figure out that he has forgotten his previous conversations with the Drifter and becomes angry with them for doing so (as he now has no memory of having asked this of them).

While he admires Warframes, describing them as "beautiful and murderous works of art", Roathe displays significant wariness of the Tenno and especially the Operator. When asked about it by The Drifter, he clarifies this is due to the fact that the Tenno were responsible for the fall of the Orokin Empire in the course of a single night. However, he also shows empathy towards them, showing no surprise whatsoever that the Tenno retaliated against the Orokin (and especially Ballas) after all they had endured (especially Margulis's execution), and remarking that their experiences ought to have turned them into "raving lunatics", considering it a miracle that they retain their humanity in spite of this. Because of this, he will attempt to work with the Drifter to create a contingency plan in the eventuality that the Lotus (who he believes to no longer be in control of her own faculties and might be manipulated by external forces, namely Albrecht) drives the Tenno to go rogue.

###### Thermostat

Over the course of conversations with Roathe, Marie, and Lyon, it is possible to influence a counter known as "Thermostat" which affects the route Roathe's story arc takes.

- Options which urge Roathe to seek forgiveness, reflect on his prior actions, or express a desire for him to change, will increase the Thermostat counter. A Thermostat value of 5 or higher (4 if romanced) will open up the possibility for Roathe to let go of his past and move forward, seeing to do better. This is referred to as his "Redeemed" route.
- Options which justify the Empire or Roathe's actions as necessary, or which state that there is no need for him to change or justify his actions to others, will lower the Thermostat counter. A Thermostat value of 0 or lower will have Roathe pursue his own goals without consideration for the opinions of others. This is referred to as his "Villain" route.

If the Thermostat counter is between 1-4 (1-3 if romanced), then Roathe's story arc will fail unless specific dialogue choices are made which bypass this check entirely.

##### Background

Lyon's grandparents (whether maternal or paternal is unknown) met in the Gaullique Revolution. He and his parents lived and worked on a farm, with a small village nearby.

Content Warning: Self-Harm, Child Abuse ▾▾

From an early age, Lyon has experienced visions, visible only to him and often terrifying in nature. The earliest example he brings up, when he was around four to six years old, was of an old, withered woman with wispy hair and missing eyes, whom he awoke to find sitting at the foot of his bed. He informed his mother of the vision that night and when, three months later, a woman matching this description was found dead in her home, Lyon's mother took what he had seen to be the work of the Devil. She would beat him with a switch while calling him "Devil" and "Sorcerer" until he could neither stand nor sleep on his back, something it is implied she did from then on with some regularity. He also mentions to The Drifter that his father was even worse, altough he doesn't elaborate on what his father did to him.

As he grew older, these visions developed into voices that he heard at all hours, whether awake or asleep. His work on the family farm began to suffer, which in turn worsened his parents' treatment of him. The visions and voices often sent him into violent fits, from which he could only shake himself through acts of self-harm.

One night, when Lyon was approximately seventeen years of age, he experienced a vision in which shadowy figures demanded he kill his parents. When he regained awareness he found himself stood at the door of his parents' room, knife in hand, and was so overwhelmed he fled into the woods - even though by his own admition he felt no love for his parents at that point, he was still unable to kill them. Terrified of what the visions had almost driven him to do, Lyon attempted to fix the problem by gouging out his own eyes, reasoning that if he could not see the visions, they would no longer affect him. His attempt failed as he lost consciousness after stabbing the knife into his left eye socket.

He awoke in the nearby parish, his injuries tended to by a visiting Brother of Lua. Overcome, Lyon told the Brother of his visions and what they had demanded of him, expecting to be condemned for his sins. Instead the Brother gave him an amulet of Lua and sent him to the Cathedral in the Cité de la Lumière, where he would stay and join the priesthood. During his stay there, Lyon was introduced to the practice of self-flagellation by one of the other Brothers, and began using it to clear his mind when the visions grew too much to bear.

At some point during his time at the Cathedral, he met Marie and the pair grew close, considering each other to be brother and sister.

When Albrecht Entrati arrived upon the Cathedral's doorstep under the identity of "Cardinal Dimanche", Lyon thought little of it until the Cardinal suddenly vanished, a little over three years after his arrival, returning a year later with an already-transformed Roathe. Entrati explained who he was, from where and when he had come, and what task he wished Marie and Lyon to carry out, along with the Protoframe serum for both Harrow and Wisp.

Both Marie and Lyon accepted the serum and the task Entrati set for them, watching over Roathe and ascertaining whether he - and by extension the Orokin as a whole - could ever be redeemed. However, their transformations had rendered the trio unable to go out in public without heavy disguises, so they were instead kept hidden within the crypts out of sight, only communicating with Marie's spectral manifestations. This continued for an unknown length of time until Roathe reverse-engineered a Helminth that brought the three of them to the Sanctum Anatomica, where they now reside within a replica of the Cathedral's crypts.

#### Lore & History

##### Background

Marie Leroux was originally born to an affluent and influential family in Gaule, though with malformed feet, rendering her unable to walk. With her parents' efforts not remedying the situation, and to keep up appearances, Marie's mother took her to Cité de la Lumière and abandoned her there, still at a young age. Marie then turned to live with the other orphaned children in the city, where she would use her disability to her advantage to make money as a beggar.

After some time, one of the Sisters of Sol found Marie and offered to take her in at La Cathédrale, which she accepted. At the cathedral, Marie would be educated and become a nun herself. Even in her new position, she would donate what she could to the children, who would eventually find their own way through life when they survived their circumstances. It was in La Cathédrale that Marie met Lyon Allard, and quickly became his close friend for how they understood each other.

When Albrecht Entrati traveled to Marie's time and became "Cardinal Dimanche", Marie felt blessed to hear his passionate sermons, and was taken by his treatment of her, which subconsciously reminded her of the father that abandoned her. These feelings became a passionate belief in Entrati, and blinded Marie to the full picture of his character. Entrati would later reveal the truth to Marie and Lyon, give them Protoframe serums, and leave Roathe — who had just become a Protoframe himself and lost many of his memories — in their care. Marie took the serum alongside Lyon, gaining Wisp's traits. Later, Roathe developed a Helminth from samples of Protoframe blood, and the Devil's Triad found their way to the present time, bringing a copy of La Cathédrale's basement with them.

#### Overview

The Scaldra are made up of human soldiers and are one of the two enemy factions encountered in Höllvania of 1999, the other being the Techrot.

Similar to the Grineer, Scaldra units possess armor, have troops similar to the riot shield Lancer (Scaldra Barbican) and the hulking unit Nox (Scaldra Dedicant), and have an identical weakness to Impact and Corrosive.

Most infantry also carry Efervon canisters on their backs which serve as weakpoints in addition to their heads.

#### Lore & History

Scaldra is a private military contractor with close ties to the Höllvanian government. In the face of the Techrot outbreak in 1999, Höllvania declared martial law and tasked the Scaldra with curbing the spread of the infestation. The Scaldra now keep the nation under a strict quarantine, locking down any roads in and out. Additionally, the Scaldra forces control the media, even limiting communications between citizens. They mainly use Efervon to fumigate districts of Höllvania to exterminate any Techrot. Despite the Scaldra's purpose to save the city, they appear to be unconcerned with any remaining civilians.

Led by the ruthless Major Neci Rusalka and Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi, the Scaldra display an unyielding sense of duty, and will even manically charge headlong into any opposing forces. Who else assumes Scaldra's wider command is unknown, but it is known that all wine in Höllvania was requisitioned by them a few days into the outbreak. Velimir Volkov II and Minerva Hendricks consider it a mercenary cult, supported by Viktor's reference to mandatory dualistic prayer sessions and ritual purification. Minerva's recent investigations led her to believe they use religion as a cover for criminal operations, at the behest of major corporations.

Throughout the city, they can be seen aggressively mobilizing against the Techrot, arriving in TI-92 armored personnel carriers, or airdropped via plane. Despite their proliferation of the corrosive Efervon chemical agent, the success of the Scaldra's ongoing containment of the outbreak is dubious at best; the Techrot continues to spread throughout the Höllvanian underground, and skirmishes between Scaldra soldiers and infested creatures often break out into the city streets.

Despite warring against the Techrot, the Scaldra may be secretly attempting to weaponize them as shown with the H-09 Efervon Tank's transformation into a Techrot creature, seemingly unbeknownst to Viktor.

#### Overview

Unlike other versions of the Technocyte, the Techrot notably displays a dependence on technology, and is capable of interacting with software and digesting code and data. Much like the Infestation of the future, the Techrot also infects humans, but may additionally coalesce electronics and components like CRT monitors, cellphones, speakers, and wiring into the mutated bodies. According to anecdotes from Leticia, certain Techrot creatures are also observed to be purely infested machinery.

In addition to its close tie to technology, the Techrot is also known to evolve at a very rapid rate - this is most easily-observed with the Legacytes, where the Techrot can "learn" new abilities and pass them over to its gestating Legacytes in retaliation to attacks.

Within its native habitat of Höllvania, the Techrot usually spreads the most within enclosed spaces in the city, such as the inside of buildings and the underground malls and subways.

The Scaldra were brought in to attempt to exterminate the Techrot from Höllvania.

#### Lore & History

Before the Orokin, before the Radiation Wars, outbreaks of the Infestation have already been recorded in history. One of the earliest known outbreaks of the Technocyte Virus occured in the city-state of Höllvania during the late 1990's. In response, the city-state enacted mass quarantine and martial law, with the Scaldra now functioning as the government. While the Scaldra have managed to control the outbreak on the surface, the underground areas of the city are still dominated by the virus.

The Technocyte was also dubbed as a treatment-resistant disease called the Testudo syndrome, "testudo" meaning "turtle". Its most notable symptom is the hardening of patches of skin as an early marker. It was also observed to be drawn to electronics, creating proxies by mashing together flesh and machine, with some creatures being purely derived from machine. The Technocyte Virus would later acquire its more famous name of Techrot after journalist Eleanor Nightingale smuggled herself into Höllvania and published the article "The Techrot Beneath Höllvanian Streets."

Although how the Techrot appeared remains to be largely unknown, it is possible it was created by the Höllvanian government itself, given later discoveries that the Scaldra have been employing the Techrot for entertainment and weaponry, and that people who asked too many questions about the disease were stated to be disposed into the infested sewers, presumably as a cover-up.

##### Creation of the Technocyte Coda

Intercepted e-mails between the music agency HitMaker Music and a Director from the Höllvanian government reveal that the Techrot has existed since at least the year 1997, where it was employed for the Technocyte Coda project. The manager of HitMaker, Gregory V, sought to use the Coda to innovate in the music industry, using the superstar boy band On-lyne as the basis for the project.

On February 1998, Gregory V delivered DNA samples of the boys to the Director, and was impressed by how quickly progress was being done on the project.

By August 1998, the Director reports that the Coda are predictable, standardized, and controlled, to which Gregory V approves, leading to the manager to further anticipate seeing them in play.

By December 1999, the Director and the manager propose to have the prototype Coda eventually replace On-lyne, starting with having the Coda conduct off-camera interviews, and they soon hope to be able to replicate the physical traits of the band.

##### Albrecht Entrati's Foray into the Plague Year

In his own vigil against the the Indifference, the Orokin scientist Albrecht Entrati would travel back to the year 1998 under the moniker of Doktor Friday, offering his aid and services to the citizens suffering from the Techrot plague in cooperation with Internation Crisis Response members like Leticia Garcia. Entrati's supposed "miracle drug" worked at a glance, but it had only worsened the spread of the disease, causing recipients of his drug to become silent carriers of the Techrot.

When Entrati reported that the Techrot strain had mutated and was becoming critically dangerous for even people who had received the drug, he lined up his "volunteers" and administered Helminth infusions instead. Most of these "volunteers" would never come to survive, but the ones that did would become a partial Warframe, otherwise known as a Protoframe. These Protoframes possessed very much the same capabilities as a Warframe, while still retaining their sanity and free will. Some of these Protoframes would later group up and form The Hex.

##### Suspicious Correspondence

Somewhere in Höllvania, a shell game development company, Digital Extremes (the in-universe counterpart), has put up waivers requesting developers for a game called WAR SECTOR. Unbeknownst to one Sam Paige, who would be hired by the company's automated screening systems, no one actually works in the Digital Extremes offices in Hollvania, and the company's recruitment process was bastardized by the Techrot to unintentionally lure in unsuspecting people by scraping off code and data. Albrecht Entrati would later comment on their failure to quarantine the Techrot within the company's offices, and criticize their attempt to employ the Techrot as an artificial intelligence to advance game development.

##### Radiation Wars

In the 21st century, the Techrot escapes Höllvania, causing a global pandemic. This provokes a nuclear response from humanity, triggering the first of many Radiation Wars; evidently, the continued war against the Techrot ended in a pyrrhic victory, with heavy casualties to both sides and much of Earth razed. The destruction caused by the Radiation Wars is suspected by Eleanor and the Drifter to be the catalyst for the rise of the Orokin, making the Techrot the proper link between 1999 and the time period the remainder of the in-game story is set in.

##### Personality

A Coda's personality only changes the communications received after logging into the game, during Duet spawn/disappearance, and during Final Confrontation (selecting lines from a pool assigned to each band member), and has no additional effects on AI behavior during gameplay.

All Coda adversaries use the same model based on their personality, but their colors will be randomly selected, and the color scheme will be shared between all five members.

##### Flavor Text

The Coda's name is randomly generated using a specific three-variable format:

[Personality]_[Variable1]_[Variable2]_[Variable3].1999

The set of three variables is shared between all Coda members - for example if the player receives a Zeke Coda named Zeke_Brigadier_Celebrity_Ransom.1999, all of their Duet Coda spawns will also use the same Brigadier_Celebrity_Ransom name.

#### Trivia

- In musical terms, the word "coda" refers to a point to return to in order to finish a piece.

According to an interview with Creative Director Rebecca Ford, the Technocyte Coda adopting the identity of On-Lyne was inspired by her experience of infecting the family computer with a computer virus by trying to download boy band music through the now-defunct file-sharing service Napster.

  - On-Lyne's section on warframe.com has a note on the bottom stating that the "fan-site" is run by a "Rebecca F." referencing her.
- The red screen and visuals the Technocyte Coda Stadium is using are similar to the All-Seeing Eye Narmer uses for their veiled units.
- The Coda naming system appears to reference the real-life Computer Antivirus Research Organization (CARO) standardized naming system for malware, used for describing the type, platform, family and variant of a malicious program, with additional suffixes providing extra details on its behavior or infection vector.

Upon converting a Technocyte Coda, they invite the player to form a "Supergroup", which is a musical group formed by already successful musicians, either as solo artists or as members of other prominent bands. The term was popularized in the late 1960s and 70s.

  - While converted Technocyte Codas can have quirks akin to Kuva Liches and Sisters of Parvos, these quirks are entirely cosmetic, as they do not have post-conversion dialogue that the quirk might affect.
- The Descendia can spawn a randomized group of Technocyte Coda as bosses, with their frontman and name derived from any active Codas the player has. The player needs to defeat all five of them, along with a number of other enemies, to proceed to the next Infernum. They can also spawn as a Solo or Duet only.
