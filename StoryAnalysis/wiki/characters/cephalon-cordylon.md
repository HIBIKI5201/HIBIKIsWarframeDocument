## Trivia

The Sacrifice Spoilers ▾▾

- Cordylon notes in entry 12 that while the Foundry otherwise cannot synthesize organic life, Infested tissue is an exception. This is of course the reason why Warframes can be made in the Foundry. However, Entry 12 was created 3 years prior to the release of the The Sacrifice, and may have been hinting at the connection between Warframes and Infestation similar to Lephantis' line "Why do you destroy us? We are your flesh." It may also have simply been a harmless explanation for why the Foundry could construct Infested weapons.
