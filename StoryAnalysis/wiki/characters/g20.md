#### Lore & History

##### Ash's Leverian

Pilio and his brother, Dom, were both members of the Orokin school of assassination known as the Scoria. While Dom was gifted in assassination, Pilio was not so, but he nonetheless revered Ash, the Scoria's patron Warframe.

During one mission to Mars, Dom was captured by the target he was meant to kill. In Scoria Doctrine, this was an unforgivable mistake, so Pilio was sent to kill both the original target and Dom, to confirm to the Scoria that his brother's fault was not part of the family. Ash was sent with him to oversee the mission, and kill Pilio if he failed. During this mission, Pilio showed restraint and fear, then disgust at the corpses of two guards he had killed. As Pilio and Ash approached the courtyard, they were attacked by reinforcements. Ash put the boy behind cover and efficiently killed the fifty guards facing them while Pilio averted his gaze from the bodies. Ash led Pilio to the chamber where the original target was staying, and was shocked to find Dom with him, in civilian clothes, sharing a drink and missing the black gem of the Scoria between his eyes. Pilio saw images in his mind, which his gem was suppressing, and Dom asked his brother to listen to the target. The target grabbed his gun, only to be restrained by Ash. But when Pilio asked them "why?", Ash released his grip and removed Pilio's gem from his face with a talon.

The Scoria gem had been suppressing Pilio's memories, and now he could recall that the target was actually his own father, Lio De Nas. Ash let Pilio decide between the Scoria and his family. Pilio dropped his knife, and his father took him and his brother away, fleeing Mars entirely, while Ash simply waited in silence. During The Collapse, all of the Scoria were killed by Ash himself. Pilio would later tell his account of the story, which was archived in the Leverian.

#### Lore

Bilsa was an Orokin of the Sectarus class. Nothing is known about her before the Empire's fall.

##### After The Collapse

At some point after the betrayal of the Tenno, Bilsa and Executor Avantus had to flee from an Orokin Tower being overrun by Infested. The first time she is seen is when she and Avantus are abandoning a group of other (presumably Orokin) survivors within the tower to save themselves. Bilsa made a comment on Avantus' decision to leave behind the other survivors, but altogether didn't seem appalled by the action, as they make their way for a hangar to escape the overrun tower.

Shortly after, they encounter a group of Grineer. At first Bilsa is excited to see them, thinking them soldiers, but when Avantus points out they are merely workers she demands they kneel before Avantus. When they find their path is blocked by Infested, Bilsa wonders aloud if they couldn't make it through on their own, but Avantus reminds her they only have pistols and no Corrupted to back them up. Bilsa then suggests the Grineer help them, but Avantus again reminds her they have no weapons - at which point the biggest Grineer in the group, Veytok, heads to a tool case (which Avantus opens for them) and took up cutting tools and safety equipment as weapons and armor.

Before Grineer start fighting through the Infested for them, Avantus reminds Bilsa that they wont be taking the Grineer with them. Bilsa is shocked that she would say such a thing while the Grineer could hear her, but Avantus reminds her that the Grineer simply are unable to comprehend much of what they're saying. The Grineer fight through the Infestation while the two of them stayed safely in the back, allowing them to reach the hanger. Avantus orders Bilsa to open the shuttle door.

As more Infested begin charging down the path that they came through, Avantus orders the Grineer to go hold them up, but they refuse to listen. When Bilsa announces that she has the shuttle open Veytok murders Avantus with a saw through the spine. Attempting to flee, Bilsa is struck and falls to her knees. Veytok tells her she now works for them and to make the ship go.

##### New Allegiance

Some unknown time after this, Bilsa was able to get in contact with a man named Alarez, informing him of her situation and that she doesn't even feel Orokin anymore. After she tells him she is a Sectarus, and being surprised to realize that he wasn't, she attempts to caution him against coming, telling him that the Grineer in command (who now calls himself Veytok) is too dangerous, that she's been helping him amass an army of Grineer.

Alarez refuses to listen, telling her that the System is in chaos with the Executors and Council being either dead or missing; Bilsa is confused as to how he's managing to pilot the Executorial Frigate without an Executor. Together they formulate a plan to trap the Grineer in the docking bay airlock while Alarez and a team board through an emergency hatch. She goes to meet them, only to be waylaid by Alarez's guardsman and a Dax. Alarez uses a genetic descrambler, to Bilsa's surprise, to scan her for a sample of her genetic code so he can have full access to the Executorial.

Alarez informs her that he has to kill her to keep her from outranking him and orders Bilsa to cut off life support to the Grineer in the airlock. Bilsa admits that she can't, informing him that the Grineer are already overrunning his ship. Before Alarez can process, Veytok drops down on the Dax, killing him instantly, and is followed by others who kill all of Alarez's guardsman, leaving only him alive.

Bilsa admits that she is now working with the Grineer, and asks him if he'll keep her company, as the Grineer aren't much for conversation. Before she can even finish some of her questions, Veytok kills Alarez, enraging Bilsa. He reminds her that they had what they were after - the ship and the lab on board - and that she doesn't need visits from others, she's Grineer now.

#### Lore

##### The Grustrag Three

The Grustrag Three were formerly regular Grineer marines who, while aboard a troop transport, suddenly slaughtered their comrades, claiming that they were growing "impatient for the killing". After being committed to the Grustrag Reconditioning Facility, Facility Director Dr. Tengus discovered that the marines suffer from a rare mutation brought about by cloning, vastly increasing their combat potential at the cost of deteriorating mental faculties.

Under Councilor Vay Hek's order, Tengus reconditioned them, with the intent of focusing their aggression towards the Tenno instead. Although mostly successful, the Three still had lingering violent behavior towards fellow Grineer. To counteract this, Tengus developed the Grustrag Bolt, a device that limited their effectiveness against other Grineer personnel. Tengus had additional Bolts created, to be used against the Tenno during the Three's operations.

With The Grustrag Three now prepared for combat, Vay Hek deployed them with the sole purpose of attacking those who supported enemies of the Grineer. However, General Sargas Ruk criticized Hek's actions, reminding Hek of the soldiers The Three have killed and warning him that they would eventually grow out of his control.

##### Once Awake

Dr. Tengus uncovered the Infested and studied them for use as possible bioweapons. Marines were used to test the specimen's combat efficiency, and it was noted that the Marines that used fire-based weaponry survived the longest.

When the Tenno investigate the so-called bioweapon, Tengus deliberately releases the Infested as a bid to destroy the Tenno. The Tenno use a Cascade Bomb to eliminate the Infested outbreak, but the Lotus warns the Infested threat has only begun.

Meanwhile, Tengus notifies the Grineer Queens and falsely claims the Tenno were responsible for the Infested outbreak and had acquired spores to use against them.

##### The Ascension

In an attempt gain favor with the Twin Queens and prove his worth, the up-and-coming young Councilor Vay Hek collaborated with Dr. Tengus to create the Grineer Ghouls - hastily-bred clones intended to be seeded from orbit onto a targeted world - who would conquer without need of reinforcements or support.

In an act of betrayal, or perhaps as a deliberate stunt to showcase their ferocity to the Queens, the treacherous Tengus kicked Vay Hek into the Ghouls' enclosure, soon torn apart by the ravenous abominations. The Queens were impressed by the display; However, Tengus was ordered to reconstruct Hek with cybernetics, turning him into the creature the Tenno eventually encounter. It is likely Dr. Tengus continued working under Vay Hek after this point, as there are several mentions of the former sending him subjects (Tenno or Ghoul Defectors) for dissection.

#### Lore & History

##### General

Sigor Savah was a Morphology Specialist that worked under Nef Anyo's Venusian terraforming expedition. He was tasked with decoding the Orokin gene record of the Vallis, cataloging and assessing whatever specimens were uncovered as Venus sprang back to life.

##### Discovering Specimen VK-7

Main article: Fragments/Ghoul

During one of his studies, Sigor came across a unique specimen of Orokin-era fauna; an unusually large kavat that was found fully intact inside a sealed closet, it's body frozen for millennia. Labeled as Specimen VK-7, Sigor returned to his lab with the kavat's body, As he was preparing for a surgical dissection, the kavat had somehow willed itself back to life.

Sigor would soon learn that VK-7 was not simply a mere kavat; it possessed a unique series of atypical behavioral characteristics. Kept in a cage within his lab, Sigor noticed that whenever a visitor came by, VK-7 would study their habits. Eventually, she came to anticipate regular arrivals, having memorized their schedules.

One day, after his fourth shift, Sigor entered his lab and noticed that VK-7's cage was empty. Reports soon returned of numerous Infested hive sites that were found destroyed, and that a large beast was sighted at others. Sigor would attempt to join up with a Corpus team deployed to a nearby hive, but when he arrived, he found that the entire team and hive were slain by the kavat. Before he was able to flee, he was blocked by VK-7. Discouraging him from calling for backup, the beast revealed her wounded side to him as an act of trust. Placing loyalty above profit, Sigor found himself tending to VK-7's wounds.

After the incident, VK-7 managed to infiltrate his lab without raising any alarms or alerting any of the guards. In her jaws, she held a severed, fossilized hand, and left without a sound, leaving the hand behind. Sigor began to analyze the hand, and discovered that it was part of an Orokin-made Warframe, one Anyo Corp had no record of in any of their treatise. Sigor soon began to extract a working blueprint of the warframe, who's name was Khora.

The Corpus, naming VK-7 as the Horror of the Hives, deemed the creature a threat unworthy of study, and declared that it was to be destroyed in its lair. Sigor would attempt to rescue her by using a canister of animal tranquilizer to stun the incoming hunting party. However, their respirators easily dealt with the gas, and he was promptly detained.

For his betrayal, Sigor was imprisoned and interrogated on everything he knew about Specimen VK-7, and soon was sentenced to be executed. As he closed his eyes, about to be shot in the head, VK-7 would then arrive, and saved him from execution. Knowing both of them could no longer stay on Venus, the pair fled for a conveyor bound for a nearby orbiting Rail tractor, but the Corpus had caught up to them. Giving the scientist one final glance, VK-7 activated the conveyor and lunged at her pursuers, sacrificing herself and sending Sigor into orbit.

Now a "better man than a scientist", Sigor would then dedicate his life to searching for Khora's remains. He found out later that her fused body was found intact, within the Orokin terraforming complex on Venus. However, she was shipped offworld by a notorious Solaris Rail agent (presumably Little Duck), who vanished right after.

##### On the Plains of Eidolon

Believing that Khora's remains were taken to the Plains of Eidolon, Sigor promptly left for Earth, leaving his journal logs in the care of his friend, Konzu. Sigor would reside within the Plains for many years, and would eventually meet Master Teasonai, mentoring him in his craft of beast-taming.

Khora's blueprints would then be donated to the Sanctuary of Cephalon Simaris by a member of The Quills. Shortly after, one of the cephalon's Sentinels found and synthesized Specimen VK-7, who appeared to have desired capture. However, the kavat immediately disappeared alongside Khora's blueprints, much to the chagrin of Simaris. He states that her presence can often be detected during Sanctuary Onslaught, and that she waits for a worthy Tenno to claim Khora's parts.

Sigor would soon gain an interest in studying the Grineer Ghouls, and would conduct interviews with the Ostron locals, who had became victims of the Ghouls' raids and disruptions. As a former researcher, he documented their behavior and ferocity in his notes. These notes were left behind after Sigor mysteriously vanished from the Plains one day.

A large amount of time has passed since his disappearance, and it is unknown if Sigor is still alive in the Origin System

#### Trivia

Samodeus' name could be possibly formed from the words Samo (Serbo-Croatian word for "just" and "only one") and Deus (Latin word for "God").

  - "Samo" is also the Proto-West Germanic word for "seed", referring to him being the creator of Riven Mods and presumably all Mods.
  - The prefix "samo-" is the word for "auto-" and "self-", both referring to the Mods being part of him.

#### Lore

Pratoo wanted Little Duck to sell arms to the Tenno in Fortuna, but she refused the offer in favor of a contract to extract Jubb Lott, a Corpus defector. She underestimated Pratoo's ability to divine the future, however. The information about the Orb Mothers that the contract revealed ultimately had her abandon treasure hunting and return to Fortuna to help Solaris United.

Now Little Duck is selling Vox Solaris wares to the Tenno, which include Amp parts, just like Pratoo wanted.
