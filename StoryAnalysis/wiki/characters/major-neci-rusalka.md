## Lore

### Along Came A Spider

Main article: Fragments/Isleweaver

Once upon a time... there was a little girl. Full of envy, she took what she didn't have. Feared everything around her and was afraid of death. Sad as she watched people fighting, lying, and her parents drifting apart. Joyed at making dancing puppets. And finally angered as she hurt others and was hurt in return.

And along came a spider who told her that he could eat everything all up for her.

But each time she turned the spider away. She let go of envy by clinging to her favorite toy and family, hid fear in the arms of strangers, turned away from sadness to happiness, turned away from joy to war, and finally turned away from war... until she realized she had nothing else left.

The spider smiled... and he ate her all up.

Fragments can be found around Scholar's Landing in the Isleweaver mode. When assembled, they tell the story of Rusalka's emotional life, which indicate that she encountered the Indifference at various points through her life, and attempted to fight it off through various means. However, most likely after having lost her arm, she ultimately gave in to the Indifference, and it possessed her.

### The Hex

By the time the Drifter arrives in Höllvania, Rusalka had already been possessed by the Indifference. She is first encountered by Arthur during a search for Dr Entrati, who warns him that the Scaldra is ordered to kill him on sight and rides off on an Atomicycle, while referring to Arthur as "kiddo".

Rusalka is then seen interrogating Entrati alongside Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi, though she tells him off when his physical force proves ineffective. Rusalka shifts between Loid and Entrati's forms to mock him, but Entrati affirms that the Indifference has a weakness to love.

When the Drifter and Aoi reach the Scaldra convoy carrying Entrati, Rusalka intercepts. Defeated, she rams her Atomicycle into the convoy, but Entrati survives. The Indifference briefly releases its hold on the weakened Rusalka, who does not recognize the Drifter; Entrati orders the Drifter to kill Rusalka and prevent the Indifference from further influencing them, but they refuse. The Indifference returns and teleports itself, Entrati, and the Drifter away.

Despite Rusalka's disappearance by the Indifference, Viktor continues to lead the Scaldra in her absence.

Eleanor reveals in a KIM conversion that she once attempted to read Rusalka's mind while she was possessed by the Indifference, having only found an abyss where the Indifference had noticed her. While the Indifference took no action, it was pleased when the encounter mentally shook Eleanor.

As stated above, Rusalka is battled inside the Mehra Tunnels as she intercepts the Scaldra convoy that carries Entrati, and attacks by shooting with her gun while dodging with her Atomicycle. The Tenno need to fend her off with the equipped Thermian RPG and shoot at her. Noticeably she takes less damage than her Atomicycle, so it is adviced to hit the vehicle to deal more damage, but is otherwise a fairly easy fight as she does not have other attacks.

### Childhood and Upbringing

Main article: Kinemantik Instant Messaging/Minerva, Velimir

Rusalka's adoptive parents Minerva and Velimir arrive in the Höllvania Central Mall in search for her. They reveal that Rusalka is an orphan of war, who was discovered by them in a bombed house while they were on an assignment. Following this, Minerva and Velimir quit their roles in the military and adopted Rusalka. While she was quite aggressive towards the two at first, she had a stuffed rabbit toy she was very attached to which Minerva used to gain her trust.

As a military prodigy, Rusalka was considered an effective leader. She once defused an armed conflict between two warlords without having fired a single bullet. She also comforted a day two cadet who lost a hand to an improperly defused bomb even though he died a month later.

Minerva is quite proud of Rusalka using her "assets" to her advantage, something Minerva herself instilled while raising her. Velimir on the other hand instantly loses his cool and takes serious offense if the Drifter hints having a sexual attraction to her. That said, her smoking habit comes from him.

When Rusalka expressed interest in joining Scaldra, Minerva showed disapproval as she had just finished investigating them but possessed heavily classified information that she couldn't reveal to either of them due to the danger it posed for all of them as a family. When Velimir approved of this, Minerva saw this as a betrayal which led to their marriage becoming estranged.

Neither Minerva or Velimir were aware that Rusalka had lost her arm.

If the Drifter fixes the marriage between Minerva and Velimir, they tell the Drifter to tell Rusalka, should they ever encounter her again: "On empty crossroads, seek the eclipse." - a phrase derived from the third orthodox book of Lua, specifically from Celestines 18:19.

### Isleweaver (Operation: Eight Claw)

Rusalka is whisked away to Duviri, bringing along The Murmur while usurping Dominus Thrax. Rusalka occasionally mocks the Drifter for leaving The Hex behind and spoke of how the Indifference offered her "life", although it is unknown how much of her words are of her own, or the Indifference speaking through her, as the Indifference implied her mind and body were already unstable and battered to begin with.

If the player finds all Isleweaver fragments, they will receive a message: "For when Sol and Lua align, the lost shall find their way home." If they also fixed the marriage between Minerva and Velimir, the Drifter reveals Rusalka's current situation with the couple explaining these are code phrases used between them to communicate safely. The couple interpret that the message is indeed from Rusalka herself and they should not worry about her for the time being.

Some time later, The Indifference's Triumph of Dust was confined in the Apocrypha, the realm of Untold Stories, along Neci Rusalka with it. Although its influence is diminished, Dominus Thrax warns the Drifter that they have a new duty to keep it contained.

## Trivia

The Rusalka are female ghost/spirits from Slavic folklore said to be ghosts of drowned women and are often malicious towards mankind.

  - Rusalka shares her namesake with a Sabotage node on Sedna.
- Her voice actress, Elsie Lovelock, also voiced Xeto from the Jade Shadows quest.

Due to her design and Corrosive Efervon harness, the community believed that Rusalka was a Protoframe Saryn. However, this rumor was debunked in Devstream 183.

  - The real Protoframe Saryn was later revealed as Minerva in Devstream 185, who is stated to be the adoptive mother of Rusalka.
- Rusalka reveals in Isleweaver dialogue that she was in a lethal stage of Techrot infection and smoked Efervon in order to stall the Techrot in her lungs. It is also revealed she lost her arm to the techrot, which The Indifference used to make a deal with her.
- While hard to notice, Rusalka wields an EFV-8 Mars during her boss fight in The Hex.
- The descriptions of the Cloning 101 and Relationship Goals posters sold by Hunhow at Pontis Tower, Uranus implies Rusalka is an artist of sorts and responsible for drawing them in-universe.
- Rusalka's right arm prosthetic visually evokes the elongated right arms of the Orokin.
