## Garmi

### 概要

Mathila の夫。本人は覚えていない

> Garmi was Mathila's husband despite her having no recollection of him (most likely avoiding the reality of her husband's death to remain happy).
> He was once keeper of the Seriglass Lighthouse on Watcher's Island which had no apparent purpose, yet Garmi kept its light burning, stating that he was keeping 'It' at bay.
> One day, however, a very disastrous Void Storm struck Duviri, causing great lengths of chain to fall from the sky (presumably the Chains of Harrow, destroyed at the end of that quest). These chains rained down destroying everything and everyone they hit, even Garmi's lighthouse.
> Koral, his daughter, mentions that her mother claims that Garmi was taken by the sea.

## Mother Opa

### 概要

商人姉妹 Calaventi の一員

> “Mother Opa of the Calaventi
> As bitter as her wine was sweet” —Mother Opa's tombstone epitaph
> Mother Opa was a member of the Calaventi merchant sisterhood of Duviri.
> The Calaventi sisterhood had villas on the Island of Manipura. There their tenant farmers grew grapes and turned them into wine, which was taken as tribute to be shared among the Dax. Thinking themselves reflections of the 'true' Orokin, they held no respect for Dominus Thrax. Their servants eventually alerted the king to their masters' insolence.

## Yimi

### 概要

Duviri の住人

> “Yimi
> Heeded not the warnings Of the Lorn shrines Haunted to her death” —Yimi's tombstone epitaph
> Yimi was a resident of Duviri.
> The Island of Lorn, one of the Lost Islands of Duviri, was notable for its shrines, many of which were dismantled, but can still be reassembled. This is not advisable, however, because it risks a local outbreak of ghosts and is thus an offense punishable by death. Despite all this, Yimi did not heed the warnings, and was slain by the ghosts.
