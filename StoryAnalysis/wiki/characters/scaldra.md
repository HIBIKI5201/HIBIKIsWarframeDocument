## Overview

The Scaldra are made up of human soldiers and are one of the two enemy factions encountered in Höllvania of 1999, the other being the Techrot.

Similar to the Grineer, Scaldra units possess armor, have troops similar to the riot shield Lancer (Scaldra Barbican) and the hulking unit Nox (Scaldra Dedicant), and have an identical weakness to Impact and Corrosive.

Most infantry also carry Efervon canisters on their backs which serve as weakpoints in addition to their heads.

## Lore & History

Scaldra is a private military contractor with close ties to the Höllvanian government. In the face of the Techrot outbreak in 1999, Höllvania declared martial law and tasked the Scaldra with curbing the spread of the infestation. The Scaldra now keep the nation under a strict quarantine, locking down any roads in and out. Additionally, the Scaldra forces control the media, even limiting communications between citizens. They mainly use Efervon to fumigate districts of Höllvania to exterminate any Techrot. Despite the Scaldra's purpose to save the city, they appear to be unconcerned with any remaining civilians.

Led by the ruthless Major Neci Rusalka and Lieutenant Viktor Vodyanoi, the Scaldra display an unyielding sense of duty, and will even manically charge headlong into any opposing forces. Who else assumes Scaldra's wider command is unknown, but it is known that all wine in Höllvania was requisitioned by them a few days into the outbreak. Velimir Volkov II and Minerva Hendricks consider it a mercenary cult, supported by Viktor's reference to mandatory dualistic prayer sessions and ritual purification. Minerva's recent investigations led her to believe they use religion as a cover for criminal operations, at the behest of major corporations.

Throughout the city, they can be seen aggressively mobilizing against the Techrot, arriving in TI-92 armored personnel carriers, or airdropped via plane. Despite their proliferation of the corrosive Efervon chemical agent, the success of the Scaldra's ongoing containment of the outbreak is dubious at best; the Techrot continues to spread throughout the Höllvanian underground, and skirmishes between Scaldra soldiers and infested creatures often break out into the city streets.

Despite warring against the Techrot, the Scaldra may be secretly attempting to weaponize them as shown with the H-09 Efervon Tank's transformation into a Techrot creature, seemingly unbeknownst to Viktor.

## Trivia

- Almost all Scaldra units, including Dedicants, can be Disarmed, prompting them to use a Machete-like weapon covered in Efervon Gas.
- The title of "Scaldra" is most likely derived from the Italian word "scaldare", which means "to heat up"/"to warm", possibly referencing cauterization. It could also be related to "scaldare" in the sense of "to irritate" or "to anger".
- Scaldra enemies are referred to as "Lasrian" in the game files, which is a callback to the Lasrian Military in Digital Extremes' previous game, Dark Sector.
- The plane used to airdrop Scaldra troops is based on the C-130 Hercules, a US 4-engine turboprop.
- The logo of the Scaldra appears to be an stylized representation of Sol (the sun) and Lua (the moon). Similar symbols can be found within La Cathédrale.

The Scaldra have several pieces of evidence indicating that they may have been predecessors of the Orokin:

  - Like the Orokin with the Infested, the Scaldra are shown to be willing to experiment with the Techrot in an attempt to weaponize it.
  - Taken further when considering Neci Rusalka's aesthetic: her mechanical right arm is longer than her left arm. This is similar to high-ranking Orokin, who tended to have elongated right arms.
  - Adding further precedence to this theory, the Warframe 1999 prequel webcomic depicts the Scaldra speaking in the Tennobet, or the Orokin language.
  - Taken even further when considering Minerva's comments about the Scaldra to The Drifter: she mentions they are bought and sold for by some of the biggest corporations in their world, whose corruption and greed run deep. She also states said corporations have a "remake the world into their oligarchic dystopia" mentality, with this being eerly similar to the dystopia the Orokin would create under their rule. With that being said, there is a corporation mentioned by Amir called "O.R.O" who owns the Nuclear Reactor in Hollvania and they are most likely The Scaldra's current employers. It is most likely their theocratic ideologies come from O.R.O.'S manipulations. This sugests that both "The O.R.O" and The Scaldra will eventually merge to become the Orokin empire in the future.

The Scaldra also possess a variety of similarities with all known factions in Warframe, which are all splinters of the original Orokin empire:

    - The Scaldra's military mass and usage of ballistic weapons, heavy or otherwise, are similar to the Grineer. Protoframe dialogue also implies that the Scaldra are incredibly numerous, again, like the cloned Grineer. This is better shown in the 1999 Demo where Rusalka remarks that Arthur will run out of bullets before she runs out of reinforcements.
    - The Scaldra's nature as a mercenary group draws similarities to both the Tenno and Corpus, who both have been known to work for questionable causes for material gain.
