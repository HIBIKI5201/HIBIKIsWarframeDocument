## Trivia

- Drillbit appears to have heterochromia, possessing two different colored irises.
- While Zeke is often presented as the frontman of On-lyne, Drillbit is actually the lead band member according to his voice actor. In real life, it is not uncommon for bands to have their lead or most popular member differ from the intended frontman.
