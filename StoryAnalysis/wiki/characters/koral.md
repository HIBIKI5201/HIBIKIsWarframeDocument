## General

A sweet and innocent girl, Koral tends to all the animals in Duviri, and possesses a fondness for adventure.

Koral is one of Mathila's children, with the other being her brother, Barris. Her father, Garmi, was the keeper of a Seriglass Lighthouse on Watcher's Island. However, during a vicious Void Storm known as the Rain of Chains, her father was lost and the lighthouse was destroyed alongside the island.

Mathila states that he was "taken by the sea", and shies Koral away from water.

## Trivia

- Like most of the other Duviri children, Koral is missing her right arm.
