## Lore

Main article: Sigor_Savah/Quotes

> “Zyl, the oxygen tech from Reclamation 3, had a previous career in psychological operations for some branch of the military. Therefore he had been selected to administer to me, to interview me, and ultimate to execute me. Barrel pressed to my forehead Zyl said he would prefer it if I did not look at him. Obliging as ever, I closed my eyes. There was a sharp crack, then nothing more. I opened my eyes. Zyl lay dead at my feet, limp, throat clasp in VK-7's jaws.”

> —Sigor Savah

Sigor Savah, once a Corpus morphology specialist, was tasked with studying an Orokin-strain Kavat, Specimen VK-7. This greatly changed him to the point that he tried to stop the Corpus hunters that came to destroy VK-7 in her lair. His plan was unsuccessful, and he was promptly arrested. Zyl's previous experience in military psychological operations led to him being the one to deal with Sigor. When it came to execution, Zyl asked Sigor to not look at him, and he obliged. When Sigor looked again, Zyl was already killed by VK-7, her holding his body by the throat.
