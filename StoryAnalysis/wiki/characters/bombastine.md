## Lore

A flamboyant actor and playwright, Bombastine suffers from extreme pride and jealousy, and is unable to love himself. He harbors deep envy for his love interest, Luscinia, whom Dominus Thrax favors more. He claims he never loved her, but when he thinks no one is listening he secretly cries over her and confesses his love for her.

### Tales of Duviri - The Covetous Courtier

> “Bombastine was envious. Of what? Anything. Wherever his gaze fell, it seemed other people had it better.”

> —Euleria Entrati

Bombastine was envious of anything and everyone. It seemed other people had it better wherever he looked. Although he was Duviri's "preening peacock", and the subject of envy by many, he never felt that it was enough and did not care for it. Many loved him, but others were loved more and that was poison to him. Even when he persuaded others with sweet words, he only ever thought how his performance could benefit him. Each new conquest was a chance to stop, but he kept indulging in a new fixation like a child instead until he eventually set his heart on the throne. Bombastine did not realize that many people suffered from self-doubt, thinking it was his alone. Not loving himself eventually made him hurl himself into disaster, and he was consumed by his Envy.

## Trivia

Bombastine could be seen in the Duviri amphitheater during Tennocon 2022, showing off some of the various surprises in Duviri.

  - In the Undercroft, a projection of Bombastine can be seen in a tile often used for Defense objectives, where he recites the same dialogue used for the Tennocon amphitheater during the lead up time to the presentation.
  - Bombastine's character is evidently altered during production, as during his debut in Tennocon 2022 he is presented as a much friendlier character with no signs of excessive jealousy.
- His name is most likely based on the word bombastic.
- His name appears in the title for a Decree, Bombastine's Malice.
- The device in front of Bombastine's chest is a microphone.
- Bombastine's Orowyrm form has a unique decoration in the form of glowing and intertwined twin snakes mounted on his head. The same twin snakes can be seen in the transmission intertwined atop his mask. Snakes represent many concepts such as envy, cunning, wisdom, medicine, deceit and poison.
