- The Dormizones are personal living spaces in the Zariman Ten Zero ship. It can be accessed after completion of The Duviri Paradox or Angels of the Zariman, the latter from the Star Chart or from an elevator near Hombask within the Chrysalith.
- Each player will have their own Dormizone space, containing a kitchen, a living room with two beds, and a large suite. It can be decorated similar to the Orbiter, with a wide variety of items including a new broad collection available from Hombask.
- However, the Dormizone cannot be made into a Base of Operations. The Dormizone also lacks all essential functions (except the Somachord) present in the Orbiter.

### Entrance

- Connecting to the elevator that leads to the Chrysalith is a small hallway with some knocked over tables and growing foliage. The equipped Companion may be found resting in one of the sides.
- A VERD-IE can be seen maintaining a small garden containing holograms of Duviri plants, which are cultivated in the paradox side of Teshin's Cave.
- If the player has completed The Duviri Paradox but not Angels of the Zariman, this area is inaccessible and the entrance door between the kitchen and hallway will be locked.

### Kitchen

- The first room of the Dormizone is a small kitchen.

#### Included Decorations

- Upon getting access to the Dormizone, there will already be decorations placed in the Kitchen that will use up decoration capacity. These can be removed and returned to the inventory. Most of these decorations can be bought from Hombask with Standing, but some can only be bought from the Market with Platinum.

### Bedroom

- Following the kitchen is a large room with two beds and two shelves.
- There are two locked doors, one of which can be unlocked upon purchasing the Vista Suite Key from Hombask, requiring Rank 5 - Angel and 100,000. The other door contains a Void doorway that grants access to Duviri, unlocked after completing the Duviri Paradox.

### Vista Suite

- This wide room contains four Warframe Displays to showcase Arsenal Loadouts and multiple shelves. The main feature is a large backdrop called Vistagraph to display landscape scenery, accompanied by Audioscapes to play ambient music. They can be purchased from the display terminal on the far right; Vistagraphs cost 75 while Audioscapes cost 50.

### Duviri

- Available after completion of The Duviri Paradox, this small room contains a Void passage leading to Teshin's Cave within the planes of Duviri. Here, the player can access their Arsenal, customize their Drifter, level up Drifter Intrinsics, review Duviri Lore, or speak to archivist Acrithis and trade for items with Duviri's resources.
