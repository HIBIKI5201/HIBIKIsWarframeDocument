- “We have learned that the Grineer Empire is preparing a major offensive against the Corpus. The Sisters have grown impatient with the Corpus monopoly on manufacturing supplies. Their aim is to destroy the resource stockpiles throughout Corpus space in order to force the arrogant Corpus back to the bargaining table. The Grineer are deploying new FOMORIAN class ships, poised to strike the Corpus within days.

You will need to inject a chemical agent harvested from the Fomorian reactors. With enough corruption the Fomorian reactors will implode and take the monstrous ship with it.” —Lotus
