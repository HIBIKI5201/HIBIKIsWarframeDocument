### Inbox Message - Operation Start

- “Operation Atramentum
- Tenno,
- I can't go to the wreck of Vesper Relay, it's too dangerous for a mere aspirant like me, but I want... no, I need to see Follie's Shadowgraph again.
- To make that happen, I need Nightmare Tatters. Find them on Vesper Relay and also in what you call Nightmare missions. These shreds are remnants of Follie's art. I think if we collect enough of those, we can reproduce a copy of her work right here in the relay. Then everyone can safely see it.
- Will you help me do that?” —Aspirant Zorba [音声](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/File:DSGInbox0011AspirantZorba_en.ogg)

### Inbox Message - Operation End

- “OPERATION ATRAMENTUM: It's done!
- Tenno,
- I can barely believe it. It worked. The Nightmare Tatters you collected have come together to create something amazing, a copy of Follie's Shadowgraph right here in the relay.
- Now everyone can see it, and I can commune with it every day. Just like my master did.
- And the best part? It can't hurt anybody.
- I can't thank you enough, but I did make a copy for you too. Display it with pride.” —Aspirant Zorba
